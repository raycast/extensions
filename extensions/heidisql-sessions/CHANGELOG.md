# HeidiSQL Sessions Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-07-29

- Search your HeidiSQL sessions and open any of them directly
- Works on Windows (registry or portable installs) and macOS (native build)
- Optional entry to open HeidiSQL without a session
- Database-specific icons for MySQL, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, SQLite, ProxySQL, InterBase, and Firebird sessions
- Port of the [Flow Launcher Heidi plugin](https://github.com/lostping/Flow.Launcher.Plugin.Heidi) by LostPing
