Logo concepts — cream palette

Same 10 designs from the original workflow, recoloured to the cream (#f4e2c4) + dark navy (#1a1a2e) palette you locked in. Each shown large, then at 56×56 on light and dark backgrounds.

Sift

Stencil Sift
A stencil-cut wordmark where each letter is sliced by thin horizontal slits, evoking a physical sifting mesh that strips away material. The geometric block letters feel sturdy and legible at small sizes, while the negative-space lines hint at formatting being filtered out into clean rows.
sift-stencil-mesh
Woven Mesh
A literal woven sieve: vertical and horizontal threads alternate over and under at each intersection, creating an honest textile weave rather than a flat grid. The rounded inner frame contains the mesh like a sieve hoop, reading clearly at favicon size while the over/under detail rewards a closer look.
woven-mesh
Funnel Stream
A side-view funnel with horizontal sieve lines visualises raw input being filtered into a single clean vertical stream below, mirroring the messy-to-Markdown transformation. Scattered dots above the opening evoke unstructured formatting being captured and refined.
sift-funnel-stream
Sieve Cascade
Varied chaotic dots above the sieve line represent raw, messy formatted input; the line itself has perforations where material passes through; below, uniform small dots arranged in a tightening triangular cascade evoke refined, structured Markdown output. The composition reads as filtration at any size.
sieve-cascade
Bisected Circle
A circle bisected by a horizontal line evokes a sieve or filter membrane — particles above (the noisy formatting) get caught while the clean output passes through. The crisp single line cutting through reads instantly at 16x16 while the small dots reinforce the sifting metaphor without clutter.
bisected-circle

Husk

Wheat Stalk
A vertical wheat stalk reduced to its essential geometry: a single stem with three symmetric pairs of curved leaves topped by oval grains. The clean, repeating pattern reads as agricultural at a glance while staying tasteful and unmistakable at favicon scale, matching Husk's act of stripping content down to its essential kernel.
wheat-stalk-minimal
Open Shell
Two concentric arcs open rightward like a husk peeled back, exposing a solid dot — the clean Markdown core revealed once the outer formatting shell is stripped. The asymmetric opening reads instantly at favicon scale while feeling ownable and geometric.
husk-shell-reveal
Peeled Pod
Two symmetrical husk halves peel outward to reveal a glowing amber kernel at the centre, evoking a pistachio or pod opening. The faint inner curves suggest the husk's hollow interior, while the central dot reads as the clean Markdown core extracted from outer formatting.
peeled-pod-kernel
Peeled H
A bold sans-serif H anchors the mark, with a peeled corner flap on the upper-right curling away to reveal the dark interior — visualising the husk being stripped off to expose what's underneath. Reads cleanly as a letterform at 80px while the peel motif communicates the core function at larger sizes.
husk-h-peel
Split Pod Seed
Two mirrored crescent shells peel apart along the vertical axis to reveal a single solid seed at the center, evoking a cracked-open pod where the husk has been stripped away to expose the essential core, mirroring how the extension strips formatting to reveal clean Markdown.
split-pod-seed
