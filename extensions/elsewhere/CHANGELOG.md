# Elsewhere Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-07-28

- Play or pause the complete Elsewhere experience from dedicated commands.
- Switch Spaces and background music tracks from focused lists.
- Turn background music on or off independently.
- Adjust ambience and music independently with dedicated 10% louder and quieter commands.
- Manage sources in the active Space.
- Open Elsewhere's main window, Sources, or Settings from separate commands.
