# ttrackii Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Timer: start a timer with a task, workspace, project, tags, and a rate (default, non-billable, or custom); stop or switch the running timer.
- Recent Entries: all your entries newest first, grouped by day, with search across task, project, and tags; continue or edit and start any entry.
- Reports: totals, change against the previous period, daily average, billable amounts, busiest day, top project, tag, and task, and breakdowns for a timeframe.
- Menu Bar Timer: elapsed time in the menu bar while a timer runs, with Stop, Edit, Start/Switch, Recent Entries, Reports, and Open ttrackii.
- Sign in through your browser with a one-time code; the session is kept in Raycast's secure storage.
