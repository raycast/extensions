# Finickizer Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Choose Browser: a chooser for links that no Finicky rule matched. Open the link once, or remember its hostname or its whole domain for a browser, after which Finicky routes it by itself.
- Manage Browsers: pick and order the apps the chooser offers, filtered to apps that handle web links.
- Manage Rules: list, reorder, retarget and delete the remembered rules.
- Patch Finicky Config and Unpatch Finicky Config: make Finicky load a generated entry config that wraps your own, untouched, config, and undo that. Two modes: open the chooser for every unmatched link, or only while fn is held.
- Reload Finicky Config: make Finicky pick up edits to your own config, by hand or automatically in the background.
