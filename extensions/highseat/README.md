# Highseat

See your [Highseat](https://highseat.com) website analytics in Raycast, and ask Raycast AI about them.

## Commands

- **View Sites** lists your sites with their visitors over the last 24 hours, and shows the selected site's figures beside the list for the period you pick.
- **View Stats** shows one site for a period: visitors, pageviews, sessions, bounce rate and revenue against the previous period, plus top pages, sources and countries.
- **Live Visitors** keeps the number of visitors on a site in your menu bar, refreshed every 5 minutes. Open the menu to see every site's live and 24-hour visitors, and pick the site to show.
- **View Goals** shows how many visitors reached each goal, and the conversion rate.
- **Add Note** pins a note to a day of a site's chart.

## AI

Mention @Highseat in Raycast AI to ask about traffic, sources, goals, funnels and revenue. With write access it can also create and change notes, goals, funnels, saved reports and site settings. AI extensions need Raycast Pro.

## Connecting

The first command or AI request opens Highseat in your browser. Pick a workspace, the sites to share, and the access:

- **Read only** lets the extension see your data.
- **Read and write** also lets it make changes. Choose this if you want AI to create notes, goals or reports.

One connection serves the commands and AI. To change your choice, run **View Sites** and use **Disconnect Highseat**, then connect again.

## Confirmations

Deletes and site settings changes ask for confirmation. Raycast's chat permission decides whether you see that prompt: on Always Allow, Raycast approves tool calls without asking.
