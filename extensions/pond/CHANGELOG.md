## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search saves by keywords or by meaning, and filter by source, folder, tag, saved view, untagged, unfiled, or the Trash.
- See recently opened saves first, with thumbnails, a detail pane, and Quick Look.
- Tag, file, rate, annotate, trash, and restore saves from the action panel, with edits that show right away.

- Browse images and videos as a grid, and search tags, folders, and saved views.

- Save the current browser tab or the URL on your clipboard, and open a random save.

- Use 14 Raycast AI tools to search, read, tag, file, trash, and open saves, plus Pond's native MCP server.
