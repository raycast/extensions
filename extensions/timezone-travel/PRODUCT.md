# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People coordinating across cities who need to compare local times quickly without leaving Raycast. Their primary task is moving one shared moment forward, backward, or directly to a typed time and immediately seeing the corresponding time everywhere else.

## Product Purpose

Timezone Travel is a focused world clock for rapid timezone comparison. Success means a user can answer "what time is that for everyone?" in a few keystrokes, with city setup kept out of the primary interaction.

## Brand Personality

Native, focused, calm. It should feel like a precise Raycast utility, with clear information density and no ornamental clock styling.

## Anti-references

No skeuomorphic clock faces, physical-material simulation, decorative dashboards, or separate controls that imply each city's time can move independently.

## Design Principles

1. One moment, many local readings.
2. Make one-hour movement and direct search equally immediate.
3. Keep city management out of the comparison flow, but make it searchable and syntax-free.
4. Show date boundaries and working hours without adding visual noise.
5. Prefer familiar Raycast behavior over custom-looking controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Inherit Raycast's theme and native focus behavior. Never use color as the only indication of working hours or day changes. Keep every movement operation available as a named action as well as a shortcut.
