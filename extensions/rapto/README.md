# Rapto

Switch windows across desktops, apply layouts, change modes and open workspaces from Raycast, powered by [Rapto](https://rapto.app), the free window manager for macOS.

## Requirements

- [Rapto](https://rapto.app) 5.60 or later, running
- macOS 26 or later

The first time you use a command, macOS asks whether Raycast may control Rapto. Allow it. You can change this later in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

## Commands

- **Switch Window**: lists every open window on every desktop, most recently used first. Type part of the app name or window title, then press Enter. Rapto jumps straight to that window, switching desktops without the slide animation.
- **Apply Layout**: lists the built-in layouts and your own. On a Manual desktop, press Enter to arrange its windows into the layout. On a Drag to Snap desktop, it picks the layout you get when you hold ⌥ while dragging a window.
- **Change Mode**: switches the current desktop between Manual, Auto Tiling and Drag to Snap. The current mode has a checkmark.
- **Open Workspace**: lists your saved workspaces with their apps. Press Enter to bring the apps back into their places. To save a workspace, arrange a desktop, then right-click it in Rapto's desktops bar and choose Save as Workspace.

Tip: give any command its own hotkey or alias in Raycast settings to open it directly.

## Privacy

The extension talks only to Rapto on your Mac. Nothing is sent over the internet.
