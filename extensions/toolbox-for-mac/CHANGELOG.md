# Toolbox for Mac Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-20

- Search Toolbox Tools: every tool from the Toolbox app (video, audio, PDF, images, text), with its options.
- Quick commands that work on the files selected in Finder: Compress Video, Compress Images, Remove Background, Compress PDF, Merge PDFs, Extract Text, Transcribe and Video to GIF.
- Download Video from a link, with quality and audio-only options.
