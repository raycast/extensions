# Hotspot Meter Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-11

- Menu bar totals for the last 15 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours and 4 hours, plus a total since your last reset
- Per-app breakdown with remote destinations, retransmitted data and what each macOS background service is
- Spike detection with the app that caused it
- Count only on a personal hotspot (auto-detected) or on networks you pick
- Continuous low-overhead stream so short-lived connections are counted too
- Network Usage Details window with per-app traffic history, destinations and spikes
- Small menu (top 5 apps per window) that opens instantly; every app is one click away in the details window
