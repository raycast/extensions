# Circleback Changelog

## [Error reporting] - 2026-10-22

- Errors shown in a toast are now reported to Raycast so we can fix them.

## [Ask Circleback and Chats] - 2026-10-15

- **Ask Circleback**: Ask Circleback about your meetings and more.
- **Chats**: Browse and continue your chats.
- Signing in now asks for access to your Circleback account, which Ask Circleback and Chats need. Existing users sign in again once.

## [Updated app URL] - 2026-07-01

- Updated the Circleback app URL from app.circleback.ai to circleback.ai.

## [Initial Version] - 2025-09-09

- Added Circleback extension
- **New Meeting**: Record a new meeting in Circleback.
- **Open Last Meeting**: Open your most recent meeting in Circleback.
- **Search Meetings**: Search your meetings in Circleback.
