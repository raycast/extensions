# SolMarrow

Schedule social media posts with [SolMarrow](https://solmarrow.com) without leaving Raycast.

- **Create Post**: write a post, pick accounts, add an image or video link, and save a draft or schedule it.
- **Upcoming Posts**: see what's scheduled, soonest first, and delete posts you no longer want.
- **Failed Posts**: see posts that didn't go out and the reason for each account.

## Setup

1. Create a free account at [solmarrow.com](https://solmarrow.com) and connect your social accounts.
2. Run any SolMarrow command and choose **Sign in with SolMarrow**. Approve access in the browser.

You can remove Raycast's access at any time in SolMarrow under **Settings → Apps and AI agents**.
