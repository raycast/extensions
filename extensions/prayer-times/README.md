# Islamic Prayer Times

A beautiful Raycast extension for accurate Islamic prayer times with countdowns, notifications, and modern macOS design.

## Features

- 🕌 **Accurate Prayer Times**: Uses Aladhan API for precise Islamic prayer times
- ⏰ **Countdown Mode**: Real-time countdown to the next prayer
- 🕐 **Time Mode**: Display the exact time of the next prayer
- 🌍 **Smart Location**: Search from 50+ major cities or enter custom city name
- 🎯 **Auto-Suggested Methods**: Calculation method automatically suggested based on your city
- 🎨 **Customizable Display**: Choose icon only, text only, or both in menu bar
- 📍 **Multiple Calculation Methods**: Support for 14+ Islamic calculation methods

## Setup

1. Open the extension from Raycast
2. Click "Configure Settings" or use the Settings menu item
3. Select your city from the dropdown (50+ major cities) or enter a custom city name
4. The calculation method will be auto-suggested based on your location, or you can manually select one
5. Choose your display preferences:
   - Display mode: Countdown till next prayer or Next prayer time
   - Menu bar display: Icon and text, text only, or icon only

## Usage

### Menu Bar

The extension appears in your menu bar showing:
- Next prayer name
- Countdown or time (switchable)

Click the menu bar item to:
- View all prayer times for the day
- See which prayer is current (marked with ▶)
- See countdown or exact time for upcoming prayers
- Access settings

## Prayer Times

- **Fajr**: Dawn prayer
- **Sunrise**: Sunrise time
- **Dhuhr**: Noon prayer
- **Asr**: Afternoon prayer
- **Maghrib**: Sunset prayer
- **Isha**: Night prayer

## Calculation Methods

The extension supports multiple Islamic calculation methods including:
- Muslim World League
- Islamic Society of North America
- Egyptian General Authority of Survey
- Umm Al-Qura University, Makkah
- And many more...

## Current Prayer

When it's time for prayer:
- Current prayer is highlighted with ▶ symbol
- Shows "(Now)" next to the current prayer time

## Requirements

- macOS
- Raycast
- Internet connection (for fetching prayer times)
