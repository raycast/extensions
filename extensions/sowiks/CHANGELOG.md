# Sowiks Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-09-05

- Capture commands: area, fixed size, fullscreen, window, scrolling, OCR text, self-timer and collection
- Screen recording: toggle, and pause or resume
- Unpin All and Open Settings
- Open Dashboard and Manage Integrations on sowiks.com
- Tells you when Sowiks is missing or too old to be controlled, with a link to download it
