# Floppy Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search Floppy: find any file on your Floppy Disk, copy its link, open it or show it in Finder.
- Share File and Share Finder Selection: turn files into one fpy.sh link, optionally for one named person, and copy it.
- Recent Links: your newest links with their views, and who read each one, page by page.
- Copy Last Link.
- Ask Floppy: questions about the text files on your disk, answered by Raycast AI.
- AI tools: ask `@floppy` in Raycast AI for a file's link or who read it.
