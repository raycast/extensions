# Trackpad & Mouse Tools Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Toggle secondary click between two fingers and the bottom right or bottom left corner with one hotkey
- Fixed commands for each secondary click mode
- Quick toggles for tap to click, natural scrolling and three finger drag
- Set mouse and trackpad speed to a level from 1 to 10, or step mouse speed up and down from a hotkey
- Left, right and middle click at the pointer from a hotkey, and hold any of them until you run the command again, click, or a few seconds pass, no extra app needed
- "Trackpad & Mouse Settings" lists every option from System Settings › Trackpad and Mouse, grouped like
  macOS: look up, click pressure, tracking speed, force click, zoom and rotate, swipe between pages,
  full-screen apps, Mission Control, App Exposé, Notification Center, Show Desktop, mouse tracking and
  scrolling speed, double-click speed, pointer acceleration, Magic Mouse secondary click and dragging style
- Changes apply immediately, without logging out
- Works with the built-in trackpad, the Magic Trackpad and the Magic Mouse
- Reset every listed setting to the macOS default
