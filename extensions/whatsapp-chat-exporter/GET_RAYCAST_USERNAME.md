# How to Find Your Raycast Developer Username

If you already have Raycast installed:

1. Open Raycast (⌘ + Space)
2. Search for "Settings" 
3. Go to "Advanced" tab
4. Look for your "Developer Name" or check the Raycast website after signing in

OR

1. Go to https://www.raycast.com/
2. Sign in (top right)
3. Click your profile
4. Your username is in the URL: raycast.com/YOUR_USERNAME

Then update package.json with your actual Raycast username.
