# Roku Remote Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- **Roku Remote**: Keyboard-driven Roku remote: type W A S D to navigate, Space for OK, see what's playing, and launch apps in one keystroke.
- **Search Movies & Shows on Roku**: Search every installed streaming channel on your Roku for a movie, show, or person.
- **Roku Menu Bar**: See what's on your TV and control it from the menu bar.
- **Roku Routines**: Create and run configurable TV routines with ordered steps, progress, and cancellation.
- **Roku TV Dashboard**: See the state and current app or input of your saved Roku devices.
- **Roku Instructions**: Preview and run supported English instructions locally, without an AI subscription.
- **Restore Previous Roku Activity**: Return the default Roku to the app or input saved before the last switch.
- **Run Saved Roku Routine**: Run a saved routine using its Quicklink or ID.
- **Manage Roku TVs**: Discover, add, name, and select your Roku TVs and players.
- **Launch Roku App**: Browse and launch apps installed on your Roku.
- **Type Text on Roku**: Send text to the active text field on your Roku.
- **Play/Pause Roku**: Send play/pause to the selected Roku.
- **Increase Roku Volume**: Raise the volume one step on the selected Roku.
- **Decrease Roku Volume**: Lower the volume one step on the selected Roku.
- **Mute/Unmute Roku**: Toggle mute on the selected Roku.
- **Go to Roku Home**: Open the Home screen on the selected Roku.
- **Power on Roku**: Wake the selected Roku TV or supported connected television.
- **Power off Roku**: Send power off to the selected Roku TV or supported television.
