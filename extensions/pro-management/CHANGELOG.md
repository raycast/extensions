# Pro Management Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-04-05

- **Search Projects**: Scan local workspace directories (`~/project` etc.), fuzzy search, and jump into your codebase using `IntelliJ IDEA`, `PyCharm`, `iTerm2`, `Fork`, or `Antigravity`.
- **Workspace Navigation**: Pin active sprints, favorite long-term repositories, and automatically track and sort by CLI usage frequency.
- **Custom Launchers**: Dynamically inject your custom IDE or external terminal launchers through preferences using simple format variables (e.g. `VSCode:code {path}`).
- **Manage AI Skills**: A Unified Agent Registry to aggregate scattered `.claude/skills` and `.cursorrules` across multiple AI environments into a global canonical pool.
- **SSOT Symlink Distribution**: End "Configuration Drift" by syncing central prompts across tools through OS symbolic links, editing once applies everywhere.
- **Skill Backup Engine**: Automatically write resilient physical clones of your skills to a customizable backup directory whenever syncing them to your global workspace.
- **Bi-Directional Action**: Push standard rules out to local Git repositories or harvest highly effective prompts and sync them to your central master directory.
