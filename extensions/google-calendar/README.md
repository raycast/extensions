# Google Calendar

See what's next on your Google Calendar and create events without opening a browser.

## Commands

- **List Events**: Your upcoming events.
- **Create Event**: Create an event with a title, time, guests and more.
- **Create Quick Event**: Type a sentence like "Lunch with Sam tomorrow at 1pm" and it becomes an event.
- **List Calendars**: All your calendars.
- **Search Contacts**: Search your Google Contacts.

## Use it with Raycast AI

Mention `@google-calendar` in Raycast AI to manage your schedule in plain language. For example, "find 30 minutes with Alex this week", "move my 3pm to tomorrow" or "what's on Friday?". It can:

- Search, create, edit, move and delete events
- Check availability and suggest times
- Respond to invitations
- Manage calendars, labels and sharing

## Setup

The first time you run a command, sign in with your Google account.
