# Moon Mansions Changelog

## [Exact sign degrees, VOC weekday and lodge palace icons] - 2026-10-04

- Show the Moon's exact degree within its sign, e.g. `Cancer 14°32'`, plus a direction arrow showing which way it is travelling through the sign
- Show the Moon's exact degree in the sidereal zodiac alongside the tropical one
- Void of Course end time now includes the weekday when the ingress lands on a later calendar day
- Chinese lodge rows in both commands show the lodge's palace animal and its icon, instead of a generic dragon

## [Initial Version] - 2026-10-01

- Show Moon Info command (Detail view)
- Moon in Menu Bar command with tap-to-copy rows
- Arab, Vedic and Chinese lunar mansions with themes
- Seven classical planets with motion
- Calendars: Hijri, Chinese pillars, tithi/masa/vara
- Show waxing/waning on the illumination and phase rows, menu-bar tooltip and copied summaries
