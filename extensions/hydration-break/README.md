# Hydration Break

Stay hydrated like you're at the World Cup. Hydration Break lives in your menu bar and calls FIFA-style hydration breaks through your day — referee whistle, crowd cheer, and a goal celebration when you hit your target.

## How it works

You set a **daily goal** (e.g. 8 glasses) and your **active hours** (e.g. 09:00–21:00). The extension splits that window into that many evenly-spaced hydration breaks. Each break fires a system notification and a referee whistle. Log a glass with **"I hydrated!"** to hear the crowd roar, and reach your goal to trigger a confetti goal celebration.

One break = one glass, so completing every break lands you exactly on your daily goal.

## Live match mode

Run **Follow Live Match** to sync to a real FIFA World Cup 2026 game. The menu bar then shows the live score and country abbreviations, and hydration breaks fire at that match's real 22' and 67' marks. It uses ESPN's public data — no API key, no setup. Stop following to return to your daily schedule.

> Note: the actual cooling break isn't exposed by any free feed, so live reminders fire at FIFA's mandatory 22'/67' marks. The menu bar refreshes once a minute, so the live score and alerts can lag by up to a minute.

## Commands

- **Hydration Break** — the menu-bar coach (runs continuously).
- **Hydrate** — log a glass instantly; bind it to a hotkey for one-tap hydration.
- **Follow Live Match** — pick a World Cup match to sync to.

## Preferences

- **Daily Goal** — glasses of water per day.
- **Active Hours** start/end — the window breaks are spread across.
- **Break Duration** — how long each break window lasts.
- **Break Alert** — notification + whistle when a break starts.
- **Crowd Cheer** — cheer sound when you log a glass.
