# Hydration Break Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Menu-bar hydration coach that spreads your daily water goal across FIFA-style hydration breaks during your active hours.
- Break alerts with a system notification and a referee whistle, once per break.
- One-tap "I hydrated!" logging with a crowd-cheer sound and a goal celebration (confetti + trophy) when you hit your daily goal.
- Optional live-match mode: follow a real FIFA World Cup 2026 game (keyless, via ESPN) and get reminded at its real 22' and 67' marks, with the live score and country abbreviations in the menu bar.
- Preferences for daily goal, active hours, break duration, break alerts, and the crowd cheer. "Reset all data" action included.
