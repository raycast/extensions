# Llyfr Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- **Search Library**: search a local BibTeX library by title, author, year, or journal. Open PDFs, view details, copy BibTeX, and open links.
- **Search Library**: add new entries from a PDF and pasted BibTeX, and delete entries (the PDF goes to the trash).
- **Search ADS**: search NASA ADS, view abstracts, and add papers to the library with their BibTeX and PDF.
