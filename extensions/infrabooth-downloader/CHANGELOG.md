# InfraBooth Downloader Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search SoundCloud tracks, playlists and albums, with your liked tracks shown before you type
- Search Online: search all of SoundCloud for tracks, playlists and albums, scroll through every result with Show All, or paste a link to open it
- Add tracks and playlists to the queue, play them now, download tracks, open or copy SoundCloud links
- Now Playing: see the current track with live progress, control playback, shuffle and volume from the keyboard, download it, and jump to any track in the Up Next queue
- Download SoundCloud Link: paste a track, playlist or album link, preview it, choose the folder and download it in the desktop app
