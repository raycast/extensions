# Petrol Price Malaysia

View the latest weekly petrol and diesel prices in Malaysia directly from Raycast.

Data sourced from [data.gov.my](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/fuelprice) (Ministry of Finance), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Features

- Current prices for RON95, RON97, and Diesel
- BUDI95 subsidized price and SKPS commercial rate
- Separate diesel pricing for Peninsular vs East Malaysia
- Weekly price change indicators (naik/turun berapa sen)
- Copy prices to clipboard
- Direct link to data.gov.my source

## How Prices Work

Malaysia's fuel prices are set weekly using the Automatic Pricing Mechanism (APM). Prices are announced every Wednesday and take effect from Thursday 12:01 AM through the following Wednesday.

Since September 2025, RON95 has a targeted subsidy under BUDI95 (RM1.99/litre for eligible Malaysians, capped at 300 litres/month). The unsubsidized market rate applies to others.
