# Base64

Encode and decode Base64 straight from your clipboard.

## Commands

- **Base64**: Type or paste text and encode or decode it.
- **Base64 Encode**: Replaces your clipboard with its Base64-encoded version.
- **Base64 Decode**: Replaces your clipboard with its decoded text.

## Use it with Raycast AI

Mention `@base64` in Raycast AI to encode or decode text in a conversation.

## Preferences

- **Default Action**: copy the result (default), paste it into the active app, or open it in your browser.
