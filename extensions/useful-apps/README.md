# The Useful Apps

Search [The Useful Apps](https://theusefulmedia.com/apps/), a hand-picked directory of 450+ Mac and iPhone apps, without leaving Raycast. Every app has a short verdict, who it's for, who should skip it and what it really costs.

## Commands

- **Search Apps**: type a name or a job, like "clipboard" or "screen recorder". Filter by category from the dropdown. Each app shows its verdict, price, devices and rating.
- **Find Alternatives**: lists the apps on your Mac that we've reviewed, then shows better picks for each one, with a line on why you'd pick it.
- **Browse App Deals**: Mac and iPhone app deals confirmed by the developer, with the discount, any code and the date it ends.
- **Find Best App**: ranked picks for 40 jobs, with the winner first.

## Shortcuts

- `⌘ ⇧ D` shows or hides details
- `⌘ ⇧ A` shows alternatives to the selected app
- `⌘ ⇧ C` copies the review link, or the discount code on a deal

## Privacy

No account or API key is needed. The extension downloads 1 public file from theusefulmedia.com and caches it. Find Alternatives reads the names of the apps on your Mac and matches them locally. Nothing about your apps is sent anywhere.

Results are ranked by our own score. Apps marked **Featured** paid for a Featured listing on The Useful Apps; that never changes their rank here or what we write about them. Some "Get It" and deal links are affiliate links, which earn The Useful Apps a commission at no cost to you.
