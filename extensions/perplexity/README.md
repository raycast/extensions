# Perplexity

Ask [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) a question from anywhere on your Mac.

## Commands

- **Ask Perplexity**: Type your question and open the answer in Perplexity.

Turn on **Use Perplexity App** in the preferences to open answers in the Perplexity app instead of your browser. Ask Perplexity also works as a fallback command, for questions typed into Raycast's root search.
