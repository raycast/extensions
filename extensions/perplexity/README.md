# Perplexity

Ask [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) a question from anywhere on your Mac.

## Commands

- **Ask Perplexity**: Type your question and open the answer in Perplexity.

## Tips

- Give **Ask Perplexity** an alias like `px`, then type `px` and your question in Raycast.
- Use it as a fallback command: type a question in Raycast's root search and send it to Perplexity when nothing else matches.
- Turn on **Use Perplexity App** in the preferences to open answers in the Perplexity app instead of your browser.
