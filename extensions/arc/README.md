# Arc

Search your [Arc](https://arc.net) tabs, spaces, history and downloads, and open new windows, tabs and easels from anywhere.

## Commands

**Search**

- **Search Arc**: Tabs, history and search suggestions in one place.
- **Search Tabs**: All your open tabs.
- **Search Space Tabs**: Tabs in your current space.
- **Search Spaces**: Jump to a space.
- **Search History**: Your browsing history.
- **Search Downloads**: Your downloads.

**Open**

- **Open New Tab**: In the current window.
- **Open New Window**, **Open New Blank Window** and **Open New Incognito Window**.
- **Open New Little Arc Window**: A quick Little Arc window.
- **Open New Easel**: A new easel.
- **Access Spaces and Favorites**: Your spaces and favorites in the menu bar.
- **Reset All Tabs in Current Space**: Resets the tabs in your current space.
- **Open Arc Release Notes** and **Open Arc Boost Gallery**.

## Use it with Raycast AI

Mention `@arc` in Raycast AI to ask about your tabs and history. For example, "which tab has the Linear issue I was reading?" It can also open windows, open and close tabs, and switch spaces.
