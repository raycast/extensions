# Pangalin Space Manager for Raycast

Check how much space is free on your Mac, and open the right page in [Pangalin Space Manager](https://pangalin.com/space-manager/?ref=raycast) without hunting for it.

## Commands

- **Free Space**: shows free and total space on your startup disk, for example "198.3 GB free of 994.6 GB". Works without Space Manager.
- **What's Taking up Space**: opens the Space Manager dashboard.
- **Scan My Mac**: starts a new scan in Space Manager.
- **Review Safe Items**: opens Space Manager's review with the safe items ticked. Nothing is removed from Raycast. You look over the list and confirm in the app, and removed items go to the Trash.
- **Find Files**: type what you want, like "PDFs from last week", and Space Manager's Find page opens with that search.
- **Open Page**: pick a page (Dashboard, Free Up Space, Chat App Media, Apps, History, System Data or Settings) and open it.

## What you need

Every command except Free Space needs Pangalin Space Manager 3.62.0 or later installed. Space Manager runs on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 13 Ventura or later; it doesn't run on Intel Macs. Free Space works without it. If Space Manager isn't installed, the extension tells you and links to the download page.

## Privacy

The extension reads your disk's free space and checks whether Space Manager is installed. It sends nothing anywhere and deletes nothing.
