# Apple On-Device AI - Raycast Extension

An extension that leverages Apple's on-device AI capabilities to enhance your text directly from Raycast.

## Features

This extension provides 8 AI-powered text enhancement commands:

- **Fix Spelling and Grammar** - Automatically correct spelling and grammar errors
- **Improve Writing** - Enhance the overall quality and readability of your text
- **Change Tone to Confident** - Make your text sound more assertive and self-assured
- **Change Tone to Friendly** - Adjust your text to have a more approachable tone
- **Change Tone to Professional** - Make your text more formal and business-appropriate
- **Change Tone to Casual** - Convert your text to a more relaxed, informal style
- **Make Longer** - Expand your text while maintaining its core message
- **Make Shorter** - Condense your text while preserving its essential meaning

## Requirements
- **macOS 26.0+** - Required for FoundationModels framework access
