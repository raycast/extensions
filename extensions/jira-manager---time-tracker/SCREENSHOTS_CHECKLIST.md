---

## 🎨 Screenshot Tips

1. **Timing**: Take screenshots when UI is fully loaded
2. **Focus**: One feature per screenshot
3. **Context**: Show enough context but keep it clean
4. **Actions**: For action panels, show them open
5. **Dialogs**: Center dialogs in the frame
6. **Data**: Use realistic but fake data
