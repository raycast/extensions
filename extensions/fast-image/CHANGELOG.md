# Fast Image Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Browse every image found in a chosen folder (and its subfolders) as a grid
- Support for PNG, JPG, JPEG, GIF, WEBP, SVG, BMP, ICO, AVIF and PDF (rendered as a page thumbnail)
- Paste the selected image directly into the previously focused app, or copy it to the clipboard
- Sort images by name, date added, date modified, file size, most recently used, or most frequently used, with your choice remembered
- Choose whether thumbnails show the full image or fill their grid cell (cropped)
- Refresh the scanned image list on demand, or automatically on a schedule (never, hourly, daily, or every launch)
