# VisiGrid Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-09-07

- Quick Calc: evaluate Excel formulas against clipboard tables (CSV, TSV, and
  space-aligned columns; money formats like `$28,500` and `(1,234)` normalized)
- Peek Spreadsheet: preview recent spreadsheet files as a grid without opening them
- Open Workbook: search recent spreadsheet files and open them in VisiGrid
