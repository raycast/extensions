# Social Downloader Changelog

## [Initial Release] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Download videos and audio from supported social platforms on Windows.
- Add multiple links, preview playlists, and select entries before downloading.
- Process up to three downloads concurrently with per-item cancellation and retry.
- Convert local files and downloaded media to MP4, MKV, MP3, or M4A.
- Automatically prepare verified yt-dlp, FFmpeg, and FFprobe components.
