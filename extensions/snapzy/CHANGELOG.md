## [Initial Release] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Launch every Snapzy capture mode: fullscreen, area, window, active window, area-and-annotate, scrolling, text (OCR), smart element, and object cutout
- Start screen and window recordings
- Open Snapzy's annotator, video editor, history overlay, cloud uploads window, settings (with a tab argument), and shortcuts overlay
- Combine images selected in Finder
- Capture History: browse screenshots and recordings in a grid, filter by type, and copy, paste, open, or reveal each capture
- Cloud Uploads: browse uploaded captures with expiry indicators and copy their links
