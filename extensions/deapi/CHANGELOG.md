# deAPI Changelog

## [Publish under the deAPI organization] - 2026-11-19

- The extension is now published under the official deAPI organization in the Raycast Store.

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-18

- Generate images, edit images, generate videos and turn text into speech with open-source models.
- Transcribe YouTube, X, TikTok, Twitch and Kick links, or audio and video files.
- Remove backgrounds and upscale images.
- Keep a history of results, follow jobs that are still running, and see your balance.
- AI tools for Raycast AI: generate an image, transcribe a link and read text aloud.
