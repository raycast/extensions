# Bolt Pattern Finder for Raycast

Quickly look up vehicle bolt patterns (PCD), center bores, and fastener specs for over 12,000+ vehicles directly from Raycast.

Powered by [BoltPatternHQ](https://boltpatternhq.com) — covering passenger cars, SUVs, and light trucks from 1992 to 2026.

## Features

- **⚡ Instant 0ms Local Search**: Search through 12,000+ vehicle records offline without network lag.
- **🔍 Multi-Token Search**: Search by Make, Model, Year, or PCD in any order (e.g. `Civic 2024`, `5x114.3`, `Ford F-150`, `BMW M3`).
- **📐 Essential Fitment Specs**: Instantly view PCD Bolt Pattern, Center Bore (CB), and Lug Nut/Fastener specs.
- **🔗 1-Click Complete Fitment Guide**: Press `Enter` to open comprehensive wheel specs, tire fitment calculators, and compatible vehicle swaps on BoltPatternHQ.
- **📋 Fast Clipboard Actions**: Copy bolt pattern (`Cmd + C`) or full fitment summary (`Cmd + Shift + C`) for wheel shops, forums, or classifieds.

## Popular Bolt Patterns Supported

- **5-Lug**: [5x114.3 (5x4.5")](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/5x114-3/), [5x120](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/5x120/), [5x112](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/5x112/), [5x100](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/5x100/), [5x127 (5x5")](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/5x127/)
- **6-Lug**: [6x139.7 (6x5.5")](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/6x139-7/), [6x135](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/6x135/), [6x114.3](https://boltpatternhq.com/bolt-pattern/6x114-3/)
- **4-Lug & 8-Lug**: 4x100, 4x108, 4x114.3, 8x165.1 (8x6.5"), 8x170, and 8x180.
