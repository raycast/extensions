# Soren Idle Changelog

## [Collection Log, Settings and Run Modifiers] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- New Collection Log: every item, creature and building you have found, 18 achievements that pay Credits, and a rare animated pet for each skill (about 1 in 10,000 tasks, +3% XP). Pets go home when you launch the Rescue Ship, so you farm them again each run. Achievements stay.
- New Settings screen: compact numbers (12.4K), a notification toggle for task stopped, run finished, bounty ready and rare find, and save copy and import.
- The away report shows XP per hour and the levels gained for each skill.
- The menu bar shows the task's progress, and lets you switch to a recently used task or resume the last one without opening the game.
- Four new Starlight perks: Bountiful Hands, Lucky Star, Thick Skin and Field Rations.
- Run modifiers (Brutal Wilds, Slow Going, Thin Pickings): take a harder next run for extra Starlight when you launch from it.
- Items now appear in the inventory grouped by the skill that makes them.

## [Initial Release] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- A sci-fi idle game in Raycast. You crashed on the alien world Soren: salvage the wreck, mine, forage, cook, brew, build and fight.
- Fifteen skills that level to 99, with per-task mastery and resource nodes that deplete and respawn.
- A Base with drones, a Hydroponic Bay, a Trading Hub, a Bounty Board and more, plus gear to craft and wear.
- Trade with the Sorenians, take bounties and fight bosses.
- Combat plays out round by round: every swing can miss, damage varies, and gear raises your hit chance and lowers the creature's. A defeat leaves you at 1 HP and costs a share of your drops, so combat is a credit sink with special drops rather than an earner.
- Build the Rescue Ship and launch off Soren to earn Starlight for permanent perks. Each run is harder and pays better.
- Every task, building and the idle camp has its own animated blue targeting-screen art.
- A menu bar ticker keeps the game running while Raycast is closed and notifies you when a task stops (macOS).
