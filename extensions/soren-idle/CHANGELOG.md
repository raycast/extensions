# Soren Idle Changelog

## [Fifteen Skills, the Rescue Ship and a Collection Log] - 2026-11-10

- Six new skills: Xenofishing, Smelting, Hacking, Exploration, Companions and Stargazing. Fabrication is now its own skill, split out of Engineering, and starts at your Engineering level. That makes fifteen skills.
- Every skill gets a new task about every 10 levels, each with its own animated scene. Tasks are named after what they work on.
- Spend each skill's mastery pool on its tasks.
- Build the Rescue Ship and launch off Soren to earn Starlight for permanent perks. Each run is harder and pays better. There are eight perks, and optional run modifiers (Brutal Wilds, Slow Going, Thin Pickings) make a run tougher for extra Starlight.
- Combat plays out round by round: every swing can miss, damage varies, and gear raises your hit chance and lowers the creature's. Fights cost materials and ammo, and a defeat leaves you at 1 HP and costs a share of your drops. Some creatures have special drops, and Wraith and Void Stalker cores are a chance. Combat is now a credit sink rather than an earner.
- The credit economy was retuned and companions can no longer be sold.
- New survivors are guided from salvaging to mining to their first craft.
- New Collection Log: every item, creature and building you have found, 18 achievements that pay Credits, and a rare animated pet for each skill that runs tasks (about 1 in 10,000 tasks, +3% XP). Pets go home when you launch the Rescue Ship, so you farm them again each run. Achievements stay.
- New Settings screen: compact numbers (12.4K), a notification toggle for task stopped, run finished, bounty ready and rare find, and save copy and import.
- The away report shows XP per hour and the levels gained for each skill.
- The menu bar shows the task's progress, and lets you switch to a recently used task or resume the last one without opening the game.
- Save import reads from the clipboard only, and launches and failures show a toast.
- Items now appear in the inventory grouped by the skill that makes them.

## [Initial Release] - 2026-11-08

- A sci-fi idle game in Raycast. You crashed on the alien world Soren: salvage the wreck, mine, forage, cook, brew, build and fight.
- Eight skills that level to 99, with per-task mastery and resource nodes that deplete and respawn.
- A Base with drones, a Hydroponic Bay, a Trading Hub, a Bounty Board and more, plus gear to craft and wear.
- Trade with the Sorenians, take bounties and fight bosses.
- Every task, building and the idle camp has its own animated blue targeting-screen art.
- A menu bar ticker keeps the game running while Raycast is closed and notifies you when a task stops (macOS).
