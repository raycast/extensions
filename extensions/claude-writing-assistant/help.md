## Get Started

1. Sign in to the [Claude Console](https://console.anthropic.com) and open **API Keys**.
2. Create a key and paste it into **Anthropic API Key**.
3. Pick your **Second Language**: text written in your primary language (English unless you change it) is translated into it, and text in any other language is translated into your primary language.

Usage is billed to your Anthropic account. You can follow it in the **Show Usage and Cost** command.
