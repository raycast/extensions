# Microsoft Teams Keys Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Open a Teams chat by email, saved contact or name, with an optional message left as a draft or sent
- Send Teams Message for one-step messages
- Teams Contacts: short names for the people you message most, with chat, message, call and video call actions
- Meeting controls from the keyboard: microphone, camera, raise hand, share content, admit from lobby, leave call – each its own command for a hotkey
- Bring Current Teams Call to Front finds the call window, also when it is minimised
- Set your Teams status: Available, Busy, Do Not Disturb, Be Right Back, Away, Offline – with a dropdown or one command per status
- Start audio or video calls
- Teams Meetings and Join Next Teams Meeting, read from the macOS calendar
- Join Teams Meeting lets you pick from what is running now, later today and tomorrow
- Join any Teams meeting link from the argument or the clipboard, with Outlook Safe Links unwrapped
- Schedule Teams Meeting fills in the new meeting form in Teams
