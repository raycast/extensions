# bLine Clipboard

Search the clipboard history that [bLine](https://bhive.software/bline) keeps in
sync across your Macs, straight from Raycast: text, links, images and
screenshots (searchable by the text inside them), from every Mac in your bLine.

## Requirements

**bLine must be installed on this Mac** (`/Applications/bLine.app`, build 63 or
later). The extension reads your history through bLine's own command-line
helper, so nothing leaves your Mac and there is no account to sign in to. It
does nothing without bLine.

## Commands

- **Search Clipboard History** — type to filter by a clip's text, its kind,
  the app it came from, the Mac it came from, or the words inside a screenshot.
  `⏎` pastes the clip, `⌘Y` opens Quick Look, `⌘⇧D` toggles the preview pane.

## Preferences

- **Preview Pane** — show the selected clip on the right (on by default).
