# X Post Screenshot Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Screenshot X Post: render the X post in the active browser tab (with its quoted post and the earlier posts of a thread) as an image and copy it to the clipboard.
- Open X Screenshot Folder: open the folder that holds the saved screenshots.
- Themes: dark, dim and light. The backdrop gradient takes its colors from the post's avatar and media.
