## Get an API key

1. Sign in at [platform.claude.com](https://platform.claude.com).
2. Go to **Settings → API Keys** and create a key.
3. Paste it into **API Key**.

Usage is billed per token to your Anthropic account, separately from any Claude or Raycast subscription.

## Use Claude in Raycast AI

With Raycast Pro, turn on **Allow AI Models** in this extension's settings to choose your Claude models and Presets in AI Chat.
