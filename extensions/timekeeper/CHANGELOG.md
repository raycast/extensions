## First version! - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

Includes 2 commands!

- `Display Current Time`
    - Simply shows your current time and Timezone in a quick HUD Popup
- `Create Discord Timestamp`
    - Convert a date and time into several discord Timestamps. You can use the time itself (ex. 4:30pm) or use natural language (ex. Tomorrow at 4:30pm). *Even better natural language support to come!*
