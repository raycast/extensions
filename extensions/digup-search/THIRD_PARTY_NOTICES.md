# Third-party notices

## DigUp

Parts of this extension are adapted from [DigUp](https://github.com/ARahim3/DigUp):

- the AppleScript that opens a recording in QuickTime Player at a moment (`src/open/moments.ts`, from DigUp's
  `Sources/DigUpApp/Moments.swift`)
- the list of code editors and how each is told a line (`src/open/editors.ts`, from DigUp's
  `Sources/DigUpApp/CodeEditors.swift`)
- how results are labeled and grouped, and how a search runs while you type (`src/search/presentation.ts`,
  `src/search/session.ts`, from DigUp's `shell/ui/panel.js`)
