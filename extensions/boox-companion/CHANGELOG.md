# BOOX Companion Changelog

## [Initial Release] - 2026-09-02

- Browse the BOOX library, notes, media, and internal storage over BOOXDrop.
- Transfer files, manage folders, export notes, and add documents to Library shelves.
- Discover BOOX devices automatically or connect to a configured address.
- View, capture, and crop the BOOX screen with the native BOOX Lens helper.
- Quick-send Finder selections and access BOOX actions from the menu bar.
