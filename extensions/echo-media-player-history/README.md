# Echo

Echo, your media memory for Mac. Remembers every song, podcast and video across your apps and browser; resume at the exact spot with a keystroke.

Summon, filter, navigate and resume, all from the keys. This extension brings Echo into Raycast.

## Requirements

[Echo](https://www.theodorehq.com/echo) for Mac, version 1.0.14 or later, running on the same Mac.

## Setup

1. In Echo, open **Settings > Raycast** and turn on **Allow Raycast Access**.
2. Press **Copy** next to **Access Token**.
3. Paste the token when Raycast asks for it, or later under **Raycast Settings > Extensions > Echo**.

Your listening history never leaves your Mac. The extension only talks to the Echo app over a private channel on your own Mac. Some cover pictures, such as YouTube thumbnails, load from the site they came from. Resetting the token in Echo disconnects Raycast until you paste the new one.

## Commands

| Command             | What it does                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Search Echo History | Search everything you have played, then play it, open it, play it from the beginning, save, pin, rename, copy its link or remove it                |
| Now Playing         | A live line in Raycast showing what is playing. Add it to Favorites. Return plays or pauses it, or picks up your last item when nothing is playing |
| Resume Last Played  | Picks up the most recent thing you played                                                                                                          |
| Play or Pause       | Plays or pauses whatever Echo is playing, including Echo's own player and browser tabs                                                             |
