# FPL Fantasy Premier League for Raycast

Your FPL team without leaving the keyboard. Everything is read from the public FPL API, so no login is needed. Set your Team ID once in the extension preferences: open [fantasy.premierleague.com](https://fantasy.premierleague.com), go to **Points**, and copy the number after `/entry/` in the URL.

## Commands

- **My Team** - squad for any gameweek with live points, provisional bonus, automatic substitutions, captain, bench, fixtures with difficulty, team value, bank, rank and next deadline. Press ⌘⇧P to see it drawn on the pitch like the FPL site.
- **Price Changes** - players ranked from most likely to rise to most likely to fall tonight, with FPL's own likelihood, progress and net transfers. ⌘⇧R flips the order, the dropdown narrows to your squad or to players who already moved.
- **Search Players** - all players with price, ownership, form and points. Filter by position or your squad, sort by points, form, expected points, price or ownership. Open a player for upcoming fixtures, recent gameweeks and season stats.
- **Fixtures** - fixtures and results by gameweek grouped by day, with difficulty ratings and your players highlighted. Open a fixture to see both squads with live stats. ⌘[ and ⌘] step through gameweeks, ⌘P opens the gameweek picker.
- **Gameweek History** - points, rank movement, transfers, hits, bench points and chips for every gameweek, plus past seasons. Open any gameweek to see the picks.
- **Mini-Leagues** - your classic leagues with rank movement, standings, and any rival's team.
- **Live Points in Menu Bar** - your gameweek points in the menu bar, refreshed every 15 minutes while matches are on, with live points and provisional bonus per player, overall rank and the next deadline in the dropdown.
- **Open My Team** - jump straight to your points page on the FPL website.

Enter always stays inside Raycast. ⌘↵ opens the same thing on the FPL website, and the few rows that can only open in the browser show a link icon.
