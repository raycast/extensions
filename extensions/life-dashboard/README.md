# Life Dashboard

Memento mori, but make it fun. Your whole life at a glance in Raycast — and in your menu bar.

## Commands

- **Life Progress** — the dashboard: life percentage with a decade breakdown, days/weeks/months lived, birthdays, weekends, Christmases 🎄, Ramadans 🌙, lunar years, all four seasons, body stats (sleep, meals, showers, heartbeats, breaths, full moons), remaining time, paychecks, your countdowns, and every milestone from the 2-0 birthday to the expected farewell — opened with a rotating quote.
- **Life Grid** — your life as a grid of months, one row per year, colored by life stage.
- **Life Progress Menu Bar** — ⌛ percentage in the menu bar; the dropdown carries the key stats, countdowns, next milestone, and a quote.
- **Toggle Menu Bar** — one keystroke to show/hide the menu bar item.
- **Create Countdown / Manage Countdowns** — count down to any date with days, weeks, months, and years remaining.

## Setup

Set your **birthday** (1995-01-01) in the extension preferences. Optional: first working day, life expectancy (default 80), paycheck cadence, week start, and toggles for quotes/Christmas/Ramadan/lunar years.

Assumptions for the fun stats: 8h sleep, 3 meals and 1 shower a day, 80 bpm, 16 breaths/min. Seasons use northern-hemisphere meteorological dates; Ramadans and lunar years use the Umm al-Qura calendar.
