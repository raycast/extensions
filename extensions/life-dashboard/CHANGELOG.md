# Life Dashboard Changelog

## [Initial Release] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Life Progress: one dashboard for your whole life — progress, days/weeks/months, birthdays, weekends, Christmases, Ramadans, lunar years, seasons, body stats (sleep, meals, showers, heartbeats, breaths, full moons), remaining time, work life, countdowns, and milestones, with a rotating memento-mori quote.
- Life Grid: your life as a grid of months, colored by life stage.
- Menu bar: progress percentage with a full stats + countdowns dropdown, and a Toggle Menu Bar command.
- Countdowns: create, edit, and delete countdowns to any date — days, weeks, months, and years remaining.
