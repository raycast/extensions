# Safari

Search your Safari tabs, bookmarks, history and Reading List from anywhere, and control Safari from the keyboard.

## Commands

**Search**

- **Search Tabs**: Jump to any open tab.
- **Search Tabs, Bookmarks and History**: One search across all three.
- **Search History**: Browse your history.
- **Search Bookmarks**: Browse your bookmarks.
- **Search Reading List**: Browse your Reading List.

**Tabs and windows**

- **Add to Reading List**: Save the current tab to your Reading List.
- **Copy URL to Clipboard**: Copy the current tab's URL.
- **Copy Title as Link to Clipboard**: Copy the current tab as a Markdown link, `[title](url)`.
- **Duplicate Tab**: Duplicate the current tab.
- **Close Other Tabs**: Close every tab except the current one.
- **New Window** and **New Private Window**: Open a new Safari window.
- **Reopen Last Session**: Launch Safari and restore all windows from your last session.

## Use it with Raycast AI

Mention `@safari` in Raycast AI to ask about your tabs, bookmarks, history or Reading List. For example, "summarize the article in my current tab" or "find the recipe I bookmarked last week". It can also open, select and close tabs.
