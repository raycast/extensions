# Mark Everything Changelog

## [Dia Page Prefill] - 2026-11-22

- Open Save Bookmark from Dia to fill in the current tab’s URL and title.

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-18

- Search your local ME library, including full pinyin and initials supported by ME.
- Save bookmarks and their details, with separate URL and title fields for each bookmark in a batch.
- Edit bookmarks, move them to Trash, and open the ME manager with configurable shortcuts.
- Check pending submissions safely after closing and reopening a command.
- Display bundled collection icons, including offline color Twemoji icons.
