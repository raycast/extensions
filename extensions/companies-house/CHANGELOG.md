# Companies House Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-08-21

- Search UK companies by name or number, with recently viewed companies on the empty search
- View a company profile: status and status detail, registered office, SIC (nature of business) codes, and accounts and confirmation-statement dates
- Browse company officers, filter by name and by whether they are in post, and follow an officer's appointments across every company
- Search officers and directors by name
- View filing history with the official Companies House descriptions, and open or download the filed document
- View charges, and insolvency cases with their dated events and appointed practitioners
- View persons with significant control, with an explanation when the register is empty — an exemption in force, a statement filed in its place, or a genuine gap
- Search the register of disqualified directors by name
- AI tools: ask Raycast AI natural-language questions about companies, officers, ownership, charges, insolvency and disqualifications
