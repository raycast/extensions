# Lantern Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Initial release of the Lantern extension for Raycast.
- Search your Mac by meaning across files, code, PDFs, photos, audio, and videos.
- Interactive detail pane with syntax-highlighted code snippets, thumbnails, and transcripts.
- Keyboard shortcuts to open at specific lines, pages, or video timestamps.
- Copy the file (`⌘C`), the matched text (`⌥⌘C`), or the path (`⇧⌘C`).
- Photos and recordings show when and where they were made (with Lantern 0.2 or newer).
- Clear states when Lantern is not installed, not running, still setting up, downloading its models or indexing for the first time.
- `⌘Y` previews like Lantern's own panel: a photo in Quick Look; a recording or video in Lantern's player from the moment of the match, the whole file so you can go back, with the transcript; `⌘Y` closes it and stops the sound (Lantern 0.2.1 or newer). The frame and transcript details are on `⇧⌘Y`.
- Category filtering with keyboard shortcuts (`⌃1` ... `⌃0`).
