# TalkRemind Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Pause Until: pause TalkRemind for 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, until this evening, until tomorrow morning, until further notice, or for a time you type (15:30, 45m, 2h); resume from the same list
- Toggle Pause: pause or resume with one hotkey, for a length you choose in the command's preferences
- Excluded Apps: see the apps TalkRemind ignores, exclude or include any installed app
- Exclude or Include Frontmost App: one keystroke for the app you are in
- Statistics: dictation share today, over the last 7 days and day by day, time saved, and the apps TalkRemind reminded you in
- AI tools: status and statistics, pause, resume, exclude or include an app
