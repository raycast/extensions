# PostWire

Write one post and publish it to all your social accounts at once, without leaving Raycast. It needs a PostWire account and its API key; a free account works.

## Commands

- **Post to Social**: write a post (it starts with the text you have selected), add a picture or video link if you want, tick the networks and publish.
- **Connected Accounts**: see the social accounts connected to PostWire and connect more.

## Setup

1. Create a free account at [postwire.io](https://postwire.io/dashboard.html?from=raycast_setup) and connect your social accounts there.
2. Copy your API key from the PostWire dashboard.
3. Paste it into the extension preferences the first time you run a command.

PostWire has a free plan; paid plans are bought on postwire.io. The extension sends your post and your API key to postwire.io only.
