# Command icons

- `claude.png`: converted from the installed official Claude app, `/Applications/Claude.app/Contents/Resources/electron.icns`.
- `chatgpt.png`: converted from the installed official ChatGPT app, `/Applications/ChatGPT.app/Contents/Resources/electron.icns`.
- `gemini.png`: official Gemini site favicon, https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_4g_512_lt_f94943af3be039176192d.png (512 × 512).

- `grok.png`: official Grok website Apple touch icon, https://grok.com/images/apple-touch-icon.png, resized to 512 × 512.

The respective providers retain ownership of their marks. These icons identify the destinations of the local commands.

`extension-icon.png` is original AI-generated artwork created for Copy to AI
Chat with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, selected by Noah Jackson and exported as a
512 × 512 transparent PNG. It depicts a clipboard and speech bubble and does
not contain any provider's mark.

`wait-for-composer` is a universal macOS executable built from the Swift source
in `native/WaitForComposer.swift`. See `native/README.md` and
`scripts/build-native.sh` for the complete build process.
