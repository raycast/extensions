# Screen Align Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-11

- Works along whichever axis macOS leaves free: vertical rulers and ↑↓ when the displays
  sit side by side, horizontal rulers and ←→ when they are stacked

- Millimeter rulers down the facing edge of every screen, with a shared reference line
- Keyboard nudging of the vertical offset, previewed instantly and written once you stop
- Snap to aligned tops, centers or bottoms, and move a display to either side
- Read-out of position, panel size, density, offset and pointer-crossing band
- Saved layouts, matched to displays by hardware UUID
