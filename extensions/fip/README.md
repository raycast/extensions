# FIP

Listen to [FIP](https://www.radiofrance.fr/fip) and its webradios from Raycast, and see which track they are playing.

## Commands

- **Start Playing** plays your default radio in your default player. Set both in the extension preferences: QuickTime Player or VLC.
- **Choose Radio** plays FIP or one of its webradios.
- **Stop Playing** stops the stream.
- **Currently Playing Track**, **Previous Track** and **Next Track** show the track, its album and cover on the radio you last started.

## Track information

Track information comes from Radio France's live metadata. Radio France doesn't publish the previous track of the Hip-Hop, Pop, Sacré français ! and Cultes webradios, and doesn't always announce the next track in advance.
