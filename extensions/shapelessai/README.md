# ShapelessAI

Schedule and publish social media posts from Raycast on every account you connected to [ShapelessAI](https://shapelessai.com): LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Telegram and Discord.

## Commands

- **Create Post**: pick an account, write the post, and choose the next free queue slot, a set time, or now.
- **Recent Posts**: browse scheduled, published and failed posts, open the live post, copy the text.

## Raycast AI

Ask `@shapelessai` things like "schedule this on LinkedIn tomorrow at 9" or "what failed this week". It lists your accounts, creates posts (Raycast asks you to confirm first) and reads your recent posts.

## Setup

1. Sign up free at [shapelessai.com](https://shapelessai.com) and connect your social accounts.
2. Create an API key (starts with `slk_`) as shown in the [API docs](https://shapelessai.com/docs/api).
3. Paste it into the extension's API Key preference.

The same account works over the [ShapelessAI MCP server](https://shapelessai.com/docs/mcp) in Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor.
