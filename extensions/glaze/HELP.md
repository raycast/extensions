# Glaze

Use **My Projects** to open or edit local projects, **Installed Apps** to launch installed apps, and the public or team store commands to discover more apps. Press **⌘R** to refresh.

## Create and edit with AI

Ask `@glaze build a countdown timer` or `@glaze add dark mode to my Timer app`. Review the prefilled request and **press Send in Glaze** to start work.

## Choose your app

Set **Target App** in the extension preferences. Auto prefers Glaze Internal when installed. Glaze must be installed in `/Applications` or `~/Applications`.

## Connect a team store

Open **Browse Team Store** and sign in with the same account as Glaze. Choose Google or Apple in the command's **Sign-In Method** preference. Use **Switch Team** for another team or **Disconnect Store Account** to sign out.

The public store does not require sign-in. Installation is completed in Glaze. If results cannot load, check your connection and refresh.
