# wlthy Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-08-13

- Net Worth: total net worth with day / month change and asset & debt totals.
- Allocation: breakdown by asset class, currency, geography or sector.
- By Country: net worth per country with per-country leverage (assets vs debt).
- Assets: searchable list of every holding, sorted by value.
- Debts: what you owe, sorted by balance.
- Net Worth in Menu Bar: glanceable net worth in the macOS menu bar, hourly refresh.
- Read-only REST key authentication; white-label / self-hosted URL supported.
