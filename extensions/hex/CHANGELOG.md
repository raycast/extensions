# Hex Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-25

- Start, stop, toggle, and cancel dictation. Start and Toggle open Hex if it isn't running
- Paste or copy your last dictation
- Search dictation history and paste or copy transcripts, before or after Hex's Output steps
- Browse screenshots, screen recordings, and annotations Hex has captured
- Take a screenshot with Hex's picker, and start or stop an annotation
- Choose whether Hex sends annotations to OpenCode or only saves and copies them
- Search meetings, read their transcripts, and start or stop a meeting recording
- Manage vocabulary, or add a term from Raycast search
- Switch or download transcription models
