## OpenStreetMap (ODbL)

Some UN/LOCODE coordinates were supplied by the Geoapify Geocoding API and derive from OpenStreetMap. Time zones for coordinates were derived with `tz-lookup` (CC0-1.0, The Dark Sky Company), whose boundary database comes from timezone-boundary-builder and is made available under the Open Database License. The online place lookup in Manage Locations queries OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, which is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL), https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/.
