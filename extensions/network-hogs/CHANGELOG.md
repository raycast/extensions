# Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-10-29

- View live per-app download and upload rates with helper processes grouped under their app.
- Inspect traffic charts with optional sample transitions, session usage, and contributing processes.
- Pause monitoring, show quiet apps, copy a summary, and quit apps from Raycast.
- Show the busiest app in the menu bar with a configurable traffic threshold.
- Ask Raycast AI about current network activity with a read-only tool.
