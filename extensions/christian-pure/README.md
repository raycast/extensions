# Christian Pure Verses and Prayers

Today's Bible verse and prayer, a verse for how you feel, and short prayers to pray aloud before a meal or a meeting, from [Christian Pure](https://christianpure.com/?utm_source=raycast&utm_medium=plugin).

## Commands

- **Today's Verse**: the day's verse with a short note on what it says. Copy it, paste it, or open the full article.
- **Today's Prayer**: the day's prayer, ready to copy or paste.
- **Verse for How You Feel**: pick a feeling (anxious, tired, grieving, grateful and 14 more) and choose from 178 verses.
- **Prayer for an Occasion**: pick an occasion (a meal, a Bible study, a church service, a meeting, school or sports, a funeral) and choose a prayer by how long it takes to pray aloud.

Verses are from the Berean Standard Bible.

## Network use

The extension asks christianpure.com for the verse and prayer text when you open a command. It sends no account or personal data. Links carry `utm_source=raycast` so Christian Pure can count visits that came from Raycast.
