# Discord Voice Control Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Toggle Discord mute and deafen from Raycast without leaving your current app.
- Check Voice Control Status command reports readiness (Discord running, Accessibility permission, shortcut configuration).
- Best-effort messaging: the extension reports the toggle was *sent* and never claims an unverified voice state.
- Configurable mute/deafen shortcuts to match your Discord keybinds.
- Optional, local-only diagnostic logging (off by default).
