# Scento Perfume

Look up perfumes without leaving Raycast. The extension reads Scento's verified perfume database through the free [Scento Perfume API](https://www.scento.com/developers): 1,600+ designer and niche perfumes, 400+ raw materials and 160+ perfume houses.

No account, API key or setup is needed.

## Commands

- **Search Perfumes**: search by perfume, house, note or mood. Open a result to read its notes, scent family, character and when it is worn, or open its page on Scento.
- **Find Similar Perfumes**: name a perfume you love and see the fragrances that share its structure, with the notes they share and how each one differs.
- **Browse Fragrance Families**: explore Scento's 14 fragrance families, from Sunlit Citrus to Oud Court, with the signature perfumes of each.
- **Search Notes**: look up a note or raw material, such as rose, oud or vetiver, and see what it smells like, what it pairs with and which perfumes showcase it.

## Actions

Every result has an **Open on Scento** action that opens its page on www.scento.com. Perfumes also offer **Show Similar Perfumes** and **Copy Name**.

## Data

Answers come from `https://www.scento.com/api/perfume/v1`. Read the [API documentation](https://www.scento.com/developers) and the [terms](https://www.scento.com/terms-of-service#perfume-api).
