# Fotcast Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Matches command: a day's fixtures across every league, grouped by competition,
  with the kickoff time or live match clock as the row subtitle and a score
  badge between the crests that is coloured by your favorite club's result
  when the match finishes.
- Favorite teams and leagues, pinned as a `★ Teams` section and one `★ <league>`
  section per favorite league, with an All Leagues / Favorites dropdown that is
  remembered between launches.
- Day navigation with ⌘], ⌘[, ⌘T and a calendar picker.
- Full-screen match view: scoreboard with crests, score, goals and red cards,
  a pitch chart of both lineups, substitutions, player ratings, plus stadium, referee,
  attendance and top match stats.
- Live matches refresh every 60 seconds.
