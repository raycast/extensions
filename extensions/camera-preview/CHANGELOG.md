# Camera Preview Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Live webcam preview rendered natively, with no flickering
- Window size preference: full screen, or a floating, draggable window in three sizes
- Resize the preview on the fly with ↑ / ↓
- Zoom in on the centre of the frame with + / −, and toggle mirroring with M
- Save a snapshot with S or record with R, into a folder of your choosing, matching what the
  preview shows including mirroring and zoom
- Mirrored image by default for a natural selfie-style preview
- Switch between cameras with ← / →, with the current camera name shown at the bottom
- "Select Default Camera" command to pick which camera the preview starts with
- Camera preference to start with a built-in, external, or Continuity Camera
- Close with Esc, Q, or a click
- Preferences for mirroring, fill mode, and the default camera
