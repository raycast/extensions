# Again

Pick one small thing a day and keep it in your menu bar. Nothing is graded.

## Set up

1. Run **Pick Today's Thing** and choose one. This also puts a circle in your menu bar. If the menu bar is full, turn off "Show today's thing in the menu bar" in the preferences of **Show Today's Thing** to keep only the icon.
2. Make it a habit by tying it to something you already do: open Raycast in the morning and pick one thing. To make it faster, give **Pick Today's Thing** an alias such as `t` in Raycast Settings → Extensions → Again. Then `t`, Tab, and your own words set it in one step.

## How it works

- Click the circle to answer: **Done**, **Did Some of It**, or **Not Today**.
- **Make It Smaller** takes the thing down in steps: minutes are halved, then "Spend 2 minutes on: …", then "Open it, nothing else: …".
- A day you leave open closes itself as "not today". The next day offers the same thing first, and lets you set yesterday right if you did it after all.
- After a few days away you get a fresh start, with the old thing offered last.
- The icon shows the state by shape and fill: empty gray circle (pick one), green filled circle (chosen, still open), half circle (some), check (done), moon (not today). After 6 p.m., if today is still open, the circle gets three dots and the tooltip says so. No sound, no notification; you can turn it off in the command's preferences.
- The only number is the days you showed up. It never goes down. No streaks, no points.

## Privacy

Everything stays in Raycast's local storage on your Mac. No account, no network, no analytics. Not a medical app.
