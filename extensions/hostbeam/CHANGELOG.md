# Hostbeam Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Beam Clipboard — send the screenshot on your clipboard to your current host.
- Switch Host — choose where beams go, or switch and beam in one action.
- Recent Beams — browse what you have beamed, with thumbnails, and copy a
  paste-ready path back to the clipboard.
- Beam Selected Files — send what is selected in Finder.
