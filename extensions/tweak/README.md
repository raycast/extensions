# Tweak — Small Tweak, Big Difference

Fix grammar, adjust tone, paraphrase, and complete thoughts — without changing what you mean. Tweak is free and the ultimate **in-line** AI companion for your Mac that preserves your original stance and intent.

## The Problem

Copy-pasting messages into ChatGPT 50 times a day is a friction-filled nightmare. Sometimes you just need a quick grammar fix, a better way to phrase a sensitive email, or a way to finish a thought when you're stuck mid-sentence.

## The Solution: In-line Intelligence

**Select text → Hit your Tweak hotkey.**

Tweak works **anywhere you type** (Slack, Email, Notion, WhatsApp, Terminal). With one key, the polished text is instantly pasted back exactly where your cursor is.

- **No Copy-Paste**: Just select and run.
- **In-line Results**: Results appear directly in your active app.
- **Global Access**: One hotkey from any window on your Mac.

## ✨ Key Features

| Command               | What it does                                                                 |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fix Grammar**       | Fix spelling, grammar, and punctuation. Zero changes to your style or tone. |
| **Complete Thought**  | Stuck mid-sentence? Finish your thought naturally in your exact tone.        |
| **Paraphrase**        | Rewrite in clear, simple English while keeping the meaning identical.        |
| **Format Digestible** | Convert long blocks of text into digestible chunks with breaks for Slack.    |
| **Enhance Prompt**    | Transform a rough idea into a detailed, tool-specific AI prompt.             |
| **Custom Slots (x4)** | **Set your own prompts** for unique tasks (e.g. "Translate", "Summarize").   |

## 🚀 Setup

1. **Install the extension.**
2. **Configure Provider**: Go to Raycast Settings → Extensions → Tweak and choose your provider (Groq - Free, OpenAI, or Anthropic).
3. **Set Hotkeys**: Assign global hotkeys to your favorite commands.
4. **Custom Prompts**: Use the 4 generic slots to **set your own custom AI behaviors** in the extension settings.
5. **Usage**: Just select any text and trigger your hotkey. Tweak automatically replaces your selection with the result.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security

Every command preserves your original meaning, stance, and intent. It makes you sound better without making you sound like someone else. Your API keys are stored securely in Raycast's encrypted preferences store.
