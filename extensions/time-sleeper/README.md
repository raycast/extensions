# Time Sleeper

Put your Mac to sleep with a countdown timer. Perfect for when you want to step away from your desk and have your Mac sleep automatically.

## Features
- **Preset timers**: Choose from 1, 5, or 10 second delays
- **Visual countdown**: See the remaining seconds in HUD overlay

## Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Sleep in 1s | Put Mac to sleep in 1 second |
| Sleep in 5s | Put Mac to sleep in 5 seconds with countdown |
| Sleep in 10s | Put Mac to sleep in 10 seconds with countdown |

## Usage

1. Open Raycast
2. Search for "Sleep in ..." 
3. Press Enter - the countdown begins

## Use Cases
- Putting the computer into sleep mode while eliminating the risk of unintended wake-ups, for example due to mouse movement.
