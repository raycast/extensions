# Display Music Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-03-21

- Show album artwork in menu bar
- Track info dropdown (song, artist, album)
- Playback controls (play/pause, next, previous)
- Copy Apple Music link to clipboard
- Click artist name to search in Apple Music
- Click album name to reveal in Apple Music
- Configurable: show/hide track title and artist name
- Background refresh every 10 seconds
