# RipClip

Import video links, browse and download clips, and open videos in the editor with a RipClip account.

- **Import Video Link**: send the link on your clipboard (or any link) to your RipClip account.
- **Browse Latest Clips**: your newest clips, grouped by video. Open one in the editor, download it or copy its link.
- **Open in Editor**: search your videos and open one in the RipClip editor.

The first command opens a browser to sign in to RipClip and approve two permissions: see your videos and clips, and import videos. The extension never spends Horizon Units and never posts anything. It talks only to https://ripclip.org.

Privacy: https://ripclip.org/privacy. Support: https://ripclip.org/support.
