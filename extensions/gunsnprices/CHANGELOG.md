# GunsNPrices Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search Calibers: 293 calibers searchable by store spellings, copy or paste the standard name, open the GunsNPrices price comparison page. Text typed in the root search is passed in as the query.
- Parse Product Title: brand, model, caliber, capacity, barrel length, rounds per box and cost per round from a listing title; copy as Markdown or JSON.
- Calculate Cost Per Round: from a price and a round count or an ammo title; per round, cents per round, per 100 and per 1,000 rounds.
