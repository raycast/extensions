# Ramadan Companion Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-02-20

- Show both Sehri and Iftar times for today with countdown.
- Individual commands for Iftar and Sehri times.
- Configurable location (City, Country) and calculation methods.
- Support for 12/24 hour time formats.
- Hijri date display and Ramadan day (Roza) detection.
- Action panel to copy times and open preferences.
