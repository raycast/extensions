# Spark for Raycast

Triage your inbox, search mail, check your agenda, and draft replies in
[Spark](https://sparkmailapp.com) without leaving Raycast. Ask Raycast AI about
your mail, calendar, and meetings, too.

Works on Mac and Windows.

## Before you start

1. Install [Spark Desktop](https://sparkmailapp.com/download) and sign in to
   your accounts.
2. In Spark, open **Settings → AI Agents** and turn on **Spark CLI Setup**.
3. On the same screen, choose what Raycast may do with each account:
   - **Read** — browse and search mail, calendar, and contacts.
   - **Triage** — also archive, snooze, pin, and save drafts.
   - **Send** — also send mail and change your calendar.

If something is missing, the extension tells you what to do and opens the
right place in Spark.

## What you can do

- **Show Inbox** — your Unified Inbox by Smart Category: Priority, People,
  Invitations, Notifications, Newsletters, and more. Reply, archive, snooze,
  pin, or answer an invitation without opening Spark.
- **Search Mail** — find emails by topic, or narrow down with filters like
  `from:anna`, `is:unread`, or `newer_than:7d`.
- **Show Folders** — every folder, label, and shared inbox, like Spark's
  sidebar.
- **Show Agenda** — today, tomorrow, or this week. Join a call, RSVP, or email
  the attendees.
- **Find Free Time** — your free slots, or the ones you share with others, and
  turn one into an event.
- **Search Contacts** — look someone up and change how Spark treats them:
  Priority, notifications, GateKeeper, and more.
- **Compose Email** — write a new email, start from one of your templates, and
  send it or save it as a draft.
- **Show Meeting Notes** — summaries and notes from your recorded meetings.

Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) on any item to see everything you can do
with it, along with the keyboard shortcuts.

## Ask Raycast AI

In AI Chat, type `@spark` and ask in your own words:

- `@spark what's important in my inbox today?`
- `@spark when am I free with Anna next week?`
- `@spark summarize my last meeting with the design team`
- `@spark draft a reply to the latest email from Bob`

Raycast AI asks before it drafts, sends, or changes anything.

### Skills

The extension comes with Spark skills: ready-made workflows that Raycast AI
follows step by step. You'll find them under **Extension Skills** in Raycast's
AI settings. Just describe what you want:

- **Morning standup** — `@spark give me my morning briefing`: today's
  meetings, unread mail from people and Priority, and what your team is
  working on.
- **Inbox zero** — `@spark help me get to inbox zero`: goes through your inbox
  by category and archives, moves, or marks done as you decide.
- **Meeting prep** — `@spark prepare me for my next meeting`: the agenda,
  related emails, who's attending, and open threads to bring up.
- **Meeting follow-up** — `@spark draft follow-ups for my last meeting`: action
  items from the notes, drafted as emails to the attendees.
- **Newsletter cleanup** — `@spark clean up my newsletters`: which to keep,
  which to unsubscribe from, and which to block.
- **Vacation catch-up** — `@spark I'm back from vacation, catch me up`: clears
  the noise and shows what needs your attention.

Raycast AI can also take on a role that fits how you work, such as executive
assistant, founder, sales rep, or team lead: `@spark act as my executive
assistant`.

## Good to know

- **Spark Desktop must be running.** The extension works through the Spark app
  on your computer, so keep Spark open while you use it.
- **You can undo a send.** Spark holds outgoing mail and calendar changes for
  a minute, so you can undo them in Spark Desktop.
- **Spark's access levels apply.** Everything the extension does follows the
  access levels you chose in Spark's settings. When you ask `@spark`, Raycast
  AI reads what it needs to answer, like any AI Chat.
