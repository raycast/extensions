# Spark Changelog

## [1.4.4] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Open in Spark opens the selected email, draft, or meeting on Windows, not just Spark's window

## [1.4.3] - 2026-11-21

- Raycast AI sends, schedules, and unschedules drafts, sets their follow-up reminders, and deletes several drafts at once
- Raycast AI sees scheduled and unsent mail in the Outbox
- Raycast AI reads your Spark Profile and Custom Instructions before writing a draft, and can mention teammates in team comments
- Needs Spark Desktop 3.33 or later

## [1.4.1] - 2026-11-17

- Inbox by Smart Category, Search Mail, Folders, Agenda, Find Free Time, Contacts, Compose, and Meeting Notes
- Ask Raycast AI about your mail, calendar, and meetings with `@spark`, including ready-made Spark skills
- Raycast AI can show attached images inside a draft's text and list events from calendars that are hidden in Spark
- Works on Mac and Windows
- Needs Spark Desktop with Spark CLI 1.4.1 or later
