# vvd for Raycast

Your worlds, one keystroke away.

- **Search Worlds** — every world you belong to. Open it, search inside it, or capture into it.
- **Search a World** — find a card, note, map or timeline by name or by the text inside it, read it in the detail pane, open it in vvd.
- **Quick Capture** — a card or a note in any world you can edit. Selected text becomes the body.
- **Connect vvd** — log in from your browser; no key to paste.

## Setup

Run **Connect vvd**, approve in the browser, done. Prefer a key? Create one under
**Settings → API keys** in vvd and paste it into the extension preferences.

Reading and editing a world's content from outside the app is part of vvd's Pro and
Studio plans; listing your worlds works on every plan.
