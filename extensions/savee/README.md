# Savee

Browse your [Savee](https://savee.com) saves, feed and boards from Raycast, search Savee's library, and copy or download images.

## Commands

- **Search Saves**: your saves or your home feed in a grid. Filter by title, source, author, color or what's in the image.
- **Search Boards**: your boards and sub boards, and the saves on each.
- **Search Library**: references from everyone on Savee.

Every save can be opened on Savee or at its source, copied as an image, downloaded, or have its colors copied.

## AI

Ask Raycast AI about your collection: "what have I been saving lately?", "find the blue posters on my Type board", "find brutalist web references on Savee".

## Setup

You need an active Savee subscription and a personal access token. Generate the token in Savee → Settings → Developers and paste it into the extension preferences.

Library searches are limited by Savee to 20 every 5 minutes and 200 a week. The extension waits until you stop typing and keeps results for an hour, so it only spends a search when it has to.
