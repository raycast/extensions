# Connect Perigon

1. Choose **Connect to Perigon** when a command asks you to sign in.
2. Complete Perigon sign-in in the browser and approve access for MCP.
3. Return to Raycast when the connection succeeds.

You need a Perigon user with at least User role in your organization. Usage is billed to that organization.

If **Search Stories** shows a permissions error, your org may not have story clusters access; use **Search News** or **Ask Perigon** instead.
