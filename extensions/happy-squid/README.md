# Happy Squid

Start and manage your [Happy Squid](https://happy-squid.com) tasks without leaving Raycast. Happy Squid is an intelligent website and app blocker: tell it what you're working on, and it keeps your distractions blocked until you're done.

## What you need

- A Happy Squid account, the same one you use in the Happy Squid apps.
- Happy Squid wherever you want distractions blocked: in your browser, on your Mac or on your phone ([download](https://happy-squid.com/download)). Raycast starts and manages your tasks; the blocking itself happens in Happy Squid.

Tasks you start in Raycast are your Happy Squid tasks everywhere. Your browser, Mac and phone pick them up just as if you'd started them there.

## Connecting your account

Open **Current Task** or **Manage Tasks** and choose one of:

- **Continue with Happy Squid**: confirm the connection in Happy Squid on your Mac, in the app if you have it and otherwise in the browser extension.
- **Sign in with Email**: enter your email address, then the code Happy Squid sends you.
- **Continue in Browser** (in the Actions menu): confirm the connection in the Happy Squid browser extension.

Raycast gets a sign-in of its own, kept in Raycast's encrypted storage. Signing out in Raycast signs out Raycast only.

## Commands

- **Current Task** shows the time left on your current task right in Raycast's search, for example `29m left · Write launch notes`. Press Enter to open it.
- **Manage Tasks** opens your current task, or lets you choose what to work on next.

## Starting a task

Choose **New Task**, describe what you're working on and pick how long. Happy Squid checks that the task is productive, just as it does in the apps. An approved task starts right away. If Happy Squid isn't convinced, it tells you why, and you can reply to explain or edit the task.

Your bookmarked and recent tasks are listed underneath, ready to start again.

## While a task runs

Pause or resume it, edit it, stop or complete it, lock it, or bookmark it for later. A task that came from Todoist or Obsidian links back to where it came from, and completing it marks it done there too.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action                           |
| -------- | -------------------------------- |
| ⌘N       | New task                         |
| ⌘⇧P      | Pause or resume the current task |
| ⌘E       | Edit the task                    |
| ⌘B       | Bookmark the task                |
| ⌘R       | Refresh                          |
| ⌘K       | All actions                      |

## Good to know

- The time left under **Current Task** refreshes about once a minute in the background. You can switch that off in the command's settings in Raycast.
- **Open Happy Squid Settings** (in the Actions menu) opens your Happy Squid settings in your browser. It needs the Happy Squid browser extension.
