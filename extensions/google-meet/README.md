# Google Meet

Start a new Google Meet from anywhere. The link is copied to your clipboard, so you can paste it straight into a chat.

## Commands

- **Create Meet**: Creates a new meeting in your browser and copies the link.
- **Create Meet and Refocus**: Creates a meeting, copies the link and returns you to the app you were in.
- **Create Meet with Specified Profile**: The same, using a browser profile you choose.
- **Create Meet and Refocus with Specified Profile**: Uses a profile you choose and returns you to the app you were in.

## Preferences

- **Open Meetings In**: your browser or the Google Meet web app. If you've added Google Meet to your Dock as a Safari web app, meetings are created there directly.
- **Set preferred browser**: create meetings in a specific browser instead of your default one, for example the one signed in to your work account.
