# Meet With Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search a Google Workspace coworker by name and view their 7-day schedule.
- Graphical day timeline with proportional blocks, side-by-side overlaps, all-day
  strip, and a "now" line.
- Google Meet join action and attendee counts.
- Favorites and last-viewed suggestions.
- Selectable event color, defaulting to the macOS system accent.
- Free/busy fallback when a calendar is not shared.
