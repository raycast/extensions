# Elec Training Revision

Practise electrical fundamentals with short quizzes, worked examples, and a local revision history. No account, API key, or subscription is required.

Explore electrical courses and further learning with [Elec Training](https://elec.training/).

## Commands

### Start Revision Quiz

Choose mixed practice, Ohm’s Law, Power and Energy, or Series and Parallel. Each session contains up to eight randomly ordered questions. Select an answer and press Return to save it and see the explanation. Press Return again to move to the next question.

Review Mistakes selects questions whose most recent answer was incorrect. Correctly answering a question removes it from that review pool. Each question is scored once per session; browsing answer choices after submitting does not change your score.

### Browse Revision Cards

Search 24 concept cards by formula, topic, or worked example. Filter by topic, copy an example, or open its source explanation.

### View Revision Progress

See how many questions you have tried, your accuracy across all submitted answers, and the concepts to revisit. Repeat attempts count towards accuracy. Reset Revision Progress removes the saved history after confirmation.

## Scope

These original practice questions cover ideal, steady-state DC circuit theory. They are intended for learning, not installation work, circuit design, regulatory guidance, or formal assessment. They are not official City & Guilds examination questions and do not reproduce BS 7671.

Concept references:

- [OpenStax College Physics 2e — Ohm’s Law](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/20-2-ohms-law-resistance-and-simple-circuits)
- [OpenStax College Physics 2e — Electric Power and Energy](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/20-4-electric-power-and-energy)
- [OpenStax College Physics 2e — Resistors in Series and Parallel](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/21-1-resistors-in-series-and-parallel)

## Privacy

Quizzes and cards work offline. Answer history is stored on your device using Raycast LocalStorage. The extension has no analytics, login, or network requests. External links open in your browser only when selected; the destination website’s privacy terms then apply.
