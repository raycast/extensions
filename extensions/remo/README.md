# Remo

Control the Roku TVs and players on your network from Raycast.

- **Remote** — every Roku button, with keyboard shortcuts
- **Open TV App** — launch any installed app from a grid of its icons
- **Type on TV** — send text to the TV keyboard
- **Play/Pause, Volume Up/Down, Mute, TV Power, TV Home** — instant commands, great with hotkeys

Your Roku is found automatically. You can also set its IP address in the extension preferences.

**Play on TV**, **Tell Remo** and **Paste Link to TV** hand off to the Remo Mac app, which finds the title and deep-links it on the TV.
