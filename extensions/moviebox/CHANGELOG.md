# MovieBox Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-07-02

### Added
- Initial release of MovieBox extension for Raycast.
- Discover trending movies and TV series directly from the Raycast interface.
- Search for specific movies or TV shows.
- View detailed metadata, posters, genres, and IMDb ratings.
- Stream video content directly to macOS media players (IINA, VLC, mpv).
- Select specific episodes and seasons for TV series.
- Choose video resolution and audio language dubs.
- Automatic subtitle loading and selection.
- Configure default media player in extension preferences.
