# Hyperliquid Markets Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search all Hyperliquid perpetual markets, including builder-deployed DEXs; search results rank by open interest
- Live price, 24h change, 24h volume, and open interest for every market
- Master–detail layout with mark/oracle price, hourly funding and funding APR, max leverage, and margin mode; ⌘D toggles a dense multi-column table
- "Top Markets" filter hides low-liquidity builder-DEX duplicates by default, with an "All Markets" option
- Favorite markets (⌘⇧F) to pin them to the top of the list
- Open any market on app.hyperliquid.xyz, copy its symbol or price, or refresh with ⌘R
- Mainnet and testnet support
