# Coffee for Raycast

Search, open and write notes in [Coffee](https://coffeetext.app), the Markdown notes app for Mac.

You need Coffee installed, which runs on macOS 15 Sequoia or later.

- **Search Notes**: find a note by title or folder, read it in Raycast, open it in Coffee, copy a `[[link]]` to it.
- **New Note**: a title and some Markdown, opened in Coffee.
- **Add to Inbox**: one line into Inbox.md, without switching to Coffee.
- **Open Today's Note**: today's daily note, from your Daily note template.
- **Search in Coffee**: Coffee's own search, with your words already in it.

Coffee keeps notes as plain `.md` files in a folder you choose. The extension reads that folder (the one Coffee is set to, or one you pick in its preferences) and talks to Coffee through its `coffee://` links. Nothing leaves your Mac.
