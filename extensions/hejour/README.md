# Hejour for Raycast

Capture notes and todos into [Hejour](https://hejour.com) — the day-based
notes app for Mac — and search every day without leaving Raycast.

## Commands

- **Append to Today** — add a line to Today's note without leaving what
  you're doing. Hejour doesn't even steal focus.
- **Open Today** — jump to Today's note.
- **Search Notes** — search every line of every day; Enter opens that day.
- **Show Open Todos** — every unchecked todo across all your days.

## Requirements

[Hejour](https://hejour.com) must be installed. Search and todos read
Hejour's local search index; nothing ever leaves your Mac.
