# Revamp

Create or redesign websites and build web apps by chatting with Revamp in Raycast. Describe what you want, preview the result, and keep improving the same project in your conversation.

## Get started

1. Install the extension and open **Ask Revamp** in Raycast.
2. Sign in to your Revamp account when prompted. Choose the account you want to connect.
3. Describe the website or web app you want, or share a public website address to redesign.

Raycast Pro is required for Raycast's AI and MCP features. A Revamp account with available credits is required for generation and refinements. Your Revamp plan's features and usage allowances apply.

## Try it

- “Create a modern one-page website for my law firm.”
- “Redesign my website to improve its look and conversion rate.” Share your public website address when asked.
- “Build a web app where clients can book appointments.”
- “Make the homepage feel more refined and add a clearer call to action.”

Revamp can follow the work and share a preview when it is ready. You can create, inspect, and refine projects without opening Studio. Use **Open Revamp** whenever you want visual editing, project management, or publishing. A preview is not a published website; publishing and custom domains depend on your Revamp plan.

## Your connection

Raycast connects directly to Revamp's hosted MCP service and manages sign-in. The extension does not ask you to paste an API key or password. Revamp receives the requests you make through its tools, and authorized tools can read, create, or change projects in the connected account. Disconnect Revamp in Raycast's MCP settings when you no longer want to use the connection.

The extension adds no analytics, local file access, shell commands, or separate intermediary service. Revamp's normal account, usage, and privacy policies apply.
