# Bonbast Raycast Extension

Live Iranian free-market exchange rates, gold coin prices, and gold market data directly from [bonbast.com](https://www.bonbast.com).

## Commands

- **Search Rates (`rates`)**: Browse and search live sell and buy prices for 28 foreign currencies, 5 gold coins, and gold/market indices (Mithqal, 18k gold, gold ounce, and Bitcoin). Pin frequently checked rates to the top of your list.
- **Convert Currency (`convert`)**: Convert foreign currency or gold coin amounts into Toman or Rial, or convert an amount in Toman or Rial into all available currencies and coins.
- **Rate in Menu Bar (`menu-bar`)**: Display a chosen live rate (e.g., USD, EUR, Gold Coin) directly in your macOS menu bar, refreshed every 15 minutes. Click to view key rates, copy rates to the clipboard, or open bonbast.com.

## Preferences

- **Unit**: Choose whether to display Iranian prices in **Toman** (default) or **Rial** (1 Toman = 10 Rial).
- **Menu Bar Rate**: Select which currency, coin, or gold rate appears in your menu bar.

## Data Source & Disclaimer

- Data is retrieved from [bonbast.com](https://www.bonbast.com). This is an unofficial extension and requires no API key.
- Quoted prices represent Iranian free-market estimates and may fluctuate rapidly or lag during high volatility.
