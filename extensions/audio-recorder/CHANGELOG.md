# Audio Recorder Changelog

## [Initial Release] - 2026-11-23

- Record microphone, system audio, or both using a native audio-only helper.
- Keep the recording controls and optional waveform inside Raycast, with normal open/close behavior.
- Show microphone/system signal levels and warn about silent recordings.
- Select the built-in microphone independently of headphone playback.
- Pause, resume and save local M4A or WAV recordings.
- Keep a live recording indicator independent of Raycast.
- Browse, play, rename, copy, reveal and move recordings to Trash.
- Recover interrupted captures and reconnect changing audio devices.
