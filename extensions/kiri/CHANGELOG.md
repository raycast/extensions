# Kiri Focus Timer Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Start Focus Block with 25, 50, 90 or custom minutes (up to 120), an optional task and a Kiri sound.
- Kiri Timer in the menu bar with pause, resume, stop and breaks.
- Start Break and Stop Timer commands.
- Focus History of the blocks finished in Raycast, searchable by task.
- Optional pause of a focus block when a call starts (off by default).
- Optional Kiri account connection to save blocks to your Kiri history and play the sounds of your plan.
