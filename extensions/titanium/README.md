# Titanium

Summarize YouTube videos using Raycast AI. Mention @titanium in AI Chat with a YouTube link to get a summary.

## Features

- **AI Summarization**: Get quick highlights and summaries of YouTube videos directly in Raycast AI Chat.
- **History**: Keep track of all your summarized videos.
- **Transcript Extraction**: Fetches transcripts automatically for the AI to process.

## How to use

1. Open Raycast AI Chat.
2. Type `@titanium` followed by a YouTube URL.
3. The AI will provide a summary of the video.
4. You can also use the **History** command to see your previous summaries.

## Requirements

- Raycast Pro (for AI Chat and Tools)
