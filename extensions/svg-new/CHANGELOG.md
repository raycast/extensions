# SVG.new Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-10-27

- Convert the image selected in Finder to an SVG saved next to it.
- Convert the image on the clipboard (a copied image file, a screenshot or a browser image) to an SVG. The SVG is saved to Downloads and copied to the clipboard.
- Remove the background from the image selected in Finder and save a transparent PNG and an SVG next to it.
- Show vectorization progress in the toast.
- Send PNG, JPG and WebP images as they are, and convert HEIC, TIFF, AVIF, GIF and BMP images to PNG on your Mac first.
- Scale images larger than 3 MB down before upload.
