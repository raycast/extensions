# Election Results

Follow a live election count from your menu bar, without opening a website.

The first elections are Brazil's 2026 presidential elections: the first round and the runoff. Results come from the public files of Brazil's electoral court, the [TSE](https://resultados.tse.jus.br/oficial/app/index.html). New elections show up on their own when TSE lists them.

## Commands

- **Browse Elections:** every election, newest first. Open one to see every candidate's share and votes, how much is counted, and the voting schedule. Use **Show in Menu Bar** to pick the election the menu bar follows.
- **Election Results Menu Bar:** a ballot box in your menu bar that shows how the count is going, and notifies you on big moments. It follows the newest election until you pick another.

## In the menu bar

The ballot box shows where the election is:

- **Empty:** the count has not started.
- **Half full, in a party's color:** votes are being counted, and that party is in the lead.
- **Full, in a party's color:** the count is done, and that party won.
- **Empty, orange:** no results yet, 3 hours after the polls closed. Something may be wrong at the source, so check the TSE site.

It only checks for news from 10 minutes before the count starts until the count is done. Use **Refresh Now** in the menu to check at any other time.

Open it to see every candidate's share and votes, how much is counted, and the voting schedule in Brasília time and your own.

## Preferences

- **Notifications** (menu bar): a macOS notification when results come in, when the lead changes, and when a winner is declared.
