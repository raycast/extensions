# Election Results Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Show the live count of Brazil's 2026 presidential elections in the menu bar, from the electoral court's (TSE) public results
- Ballot-box icon: empty before the count, half full in the leading party's color while counting, full in the winner's color when done
- Browse Elections command: every election newest first, with candidates, share, votes and schedule, and a choice of which one the menu bar follows
- Voting schedule in Brasília time and your own time zone
- Notifications when results come in, when the lead changes, and when a winner is declared
- Checks for news only while the count runs, and warns when results are 3 hours late
