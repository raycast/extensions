# Arca Changelog

## [Embed Documents, Mindmaps & Projects] - 2026-06-12

- Embed documents, mindmaps & projects inline with compact embed cards
- Click a card to open the linked resource

## [Task Mention Support] - 2026-05-16

- Task mentions in task descriptions are now properly rendered as readable references (e.g. `Task Title (#WORKSPACE-42)`) instead of raw HTML

## [Initial Version] - 2026-05-15

Initial release of the Arca Raycast extension, allowing users to manage their Arca tasks directly from Raycast.
