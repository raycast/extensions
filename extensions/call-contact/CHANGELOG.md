# Call Contact Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-01-27

### Features
- **Swift Integration**: Ultra-fast contact searching using native `Contacts.framework` (handles 10,000+ contacts).
- **Smart Sorting**: Contacts are ranked by both "Pinned" status and frequency/recency of interaction.
- **Multi-Channel Support**: Support for Phone Calls, FaceTime (Audio/Video), and WhatsApp.
- **Priority Call**: Send a pre-filled urgent SMS and trigger a call with one shortcut (`Cmd+Shift+P`).
- **Contact Details**: Search by Name, Nickname, or Company and see contact images directly in Raycast.
- **Privacy First**: All searching and frequency tracking stays local on your Mac.
