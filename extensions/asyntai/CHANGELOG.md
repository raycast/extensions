# Asyntai AI Chatbot Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-09-29

- Recent Chats: the newest chats on your website, with the whole conversation one key away.
- Leads: every visitor who left an email address or a phone number, with copy actions.
- Tickets: your support tickets, with the status, the visitor and the conversation.
- Ask My Chatbot: ask your own AI chatbot a question and read the answer.
