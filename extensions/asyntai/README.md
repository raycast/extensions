# Asyntai AI Chatbot for Raycast

[Asyntai](https://asyntai.com) is an AI chat agent for your website. It answers your visitors from your own content, collects leads and escalates to your team. This extension brings that work to Raycast, so you read chats and leads without opening the browser.

## Commands

- **Recent Chats**: the newest chats on your website. Press Enter to read the whole conversation, message by message, with the country and the topic.
- **Leads**: every visitor who left an email address or a phone number, with the page they were on. Copy the email or the phone with one key.
- **Tickets**: your support tickets, with the number, the status and the visitor.
- **Ask My Chatbot**: type a question and read the answer your chatbot gives your visitors. Ask a follow-up in the same conversation.

Every command has an action to open the chat in Asyntai.

## Setup

1. Sign in to Asyntai, open **Settings**, then **API**, and copy your API key.
2. Run any Asyntai command in Raycast. It asks for the key once.

The extension is free. It uses the Asyntai API, which needs an Asyntai account on the Starter plan or higher.

Documentation: https://asyntai.com/documentation/integrations/raycast/
