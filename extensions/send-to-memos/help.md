# Connect to Memos

[Memos](https://github.com/usememos/memos) is an open-source, self-hosted note-taking app. You need an existing Memos instance.

1. Enter your instance's website URL, for example `https://memos.example.com`.
2. Sign in to Memos and create an access token in **Settings → Access Tokens**. The location and wording may vary by Memos version.
3. Paste the token into **Access Token**. This is not your account password.

Your memo content and token are sent directly to your configured instance. New memos are private by default.
