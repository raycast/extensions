# ScopeLock

> **Scope Creep Audit & Change Order Generator for Software Engineers & Agencies**

ScopeLock helps freelance developers, consultants, and software engineering studios track scope drift, quantify unbilled feature requests, and generate clear, professional change orders directly from Raycast.

---

## ⚡ Features & Commands

1. **Calculate Scope Creep Exposure** (`audit-scope-exposure`):
   - Quantifies unbilled engineering hours and monetary exposure in real time.
   - Accepts your agreed baseline Statement of Work (SOW) alongside new feature requests.
   - Formats a comprehensive scope exposure audit report ready to copy to your clipboard.
   - *AI-Powered*: When Raycast AI is enabled, generates a diplomatic client negotiation summary and response email.

2. **Generate Change Order Notice** (`generate-change-order`):
   - Generates formal Master Services Agreement (MSA) engineering change order notices.
   - Automatically computes reference numbers and dates.
   - Formats clean milestone budgets and bilateral approval terms.
   - *AI-Powered*: When Raycast AI is enabled, drafts a complete B2B change order agreement with formal acceptance sign-off blocks.

3. **Quick Copy Scope Notice** (`copy-scope-notice`):
   - Instant single-action copy of a standard non-waiver engineering scope defense clause directly to your clipboard for Slack, email, or ticketing discussions.

---

## 📖 How to Use

- Launch Raycast and search for **Calculate Scope Creep Exposure** or **Generate Change Order Notice**.
- Enter your client's name, requested additions, estimated hours, and billing rate.
- Press **Enter** to copy the formatted document to your clipboard.
- Use **AI Scope Analysis** (Cmd+Enter or via action panel) if Raycast AI is available on your account to generate customized client emails.
