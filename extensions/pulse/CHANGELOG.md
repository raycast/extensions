# Pulse Changelog

## [Favorites, Menu Bar and AI] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Favorites: pin any symbol with `⌘⇧P` in Search Symbol; favorites lead the list and can be reordered.
- Favorites in Menu Bar: a new command that shows your favorites' prices and changes in the menu bar, refreshed every five minutes.
- AI tools: search symbols, get quotes, and list, add or remove favorites from Raycast AI with `@pulse`.

## [Initial Version] - 2026-11-11

- Search Symbol: find stocks, ETFs, indices and Binance Spot pairs by ticker or by name in English, Chinese, Japanese or Korean, with price, change and today's chart.
