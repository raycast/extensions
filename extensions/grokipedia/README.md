# Grokipedia

Search Grokipedia and read its articles in Raycast on macOS and Windows. No account or API key is required.

## Search and read articles

Open **Search Grokipedia** and enter a topic. Select a result to read the article, follow its source citations, open it in your browser, or copy its title, content, or link.

Use **Copy All Citations** to copy the article's source URLs. Its shortcut is Command+Shift+C on macOS and Ctrl+Shift+C on Windows.

If a request fails, use the retry action or open Grokipedia in your browser from the action panel.

## Raycast AI

Mention `@grokipedia` in Quick AI or AI Chat to find articles and read their content and citations. Raycast AI availability depends on your Raycast plan and app version.

- "@grokipedia Find articles about Raycast"
- "@grokipedia Summarize https://grokipedia.com/page/Raycast_software and include its sources"
- "@grokipedia Compare Grokipedia's articles about Python and JavaScript"

The AI tools are **Search Articles**, which returns article links and snippets with pagination, and **Get Article**, which returns the full article Markdown and source citations.

Grokipedia content is generated by AI. Follow the cited sources when checking a claim.
