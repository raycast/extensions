# GuestVine

Find every podcast someone has been a guest on, straight from Raycast. Powered by [GuestVine](https://guestvine.fm), which watches thousands of podcast feeds and tracks guest appearances by person: follow people, not shows.

## Commands

- **Search Podcast Guests**: type a name to find a person (a short context line tells namesakes apart), then press Enter to see their recent guest appearances with show, episode and date. From there you can open the episode, open their GuestVine page, or copy either link.
- **Get New Appearances in My Podcast App** (action, ⌘N): opens a GuestVine setup link for that person. Sign in, tap your podcast app, and every new episode they guest on arrives in a private feed, with email alerts too. Free for up to 3 people.

No account, API key or setup is needed to search.
