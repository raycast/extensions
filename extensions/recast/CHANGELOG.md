## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Rewrite selected or entered text with Claude on Amazon Bedrock using your own AWS account.
- Choose Friendly, Professional, Concise, Polished, or Neutral, or provide a custom rewrite or translation instruction.
- Stream the rewrite, compare the original, copy or paste the completed result, and request another version.
- Cancel pending requests and retry without losing the source text or last completed rewrite.
- Use AWS SDK credentials, named profiles (including SSO and credential_process), or environment variables.
- Configure model, source Region, inference profile, response budget, and optional temperature.
- Start with Claude Haiku 5.5 through the Global inference profile.
- Keep previews literal, reject incomplete output, and recover from configuration, authentication, access, and service errors.
