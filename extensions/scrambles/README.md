# Scrambles
Get scrambles for your 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, Square-1, and more twisty puzzles directly from Raycast. Quick and completely offline.

## Usage
Search for the `Scrambles` command and open it. You'll see a list of puzzles on the left, and a scramble for that puzzle on the right.

Start typing to filter by puzzle. Press Enter to copy that scramble to your clipboard, or ⌘ + Enter (Ctrl + Enter on Windows) to generate another.

## Supported Puzzles
- 2x2
- 3x3
- 4x4
- 5x5
- 6x6
- 7x7
- Clock
- Megaminx
- Pyraminx
- Skewb
- Square-1
- And a ton of scramble variations for the 3x3, such as 2GLL, CMLL, FMC, LL, PLL, etc.
