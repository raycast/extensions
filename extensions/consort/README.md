# Consort

Control [Consort](https://consortaudio.com) directly from Raycast on macOS.

Pair any two AirPlay, Bluetooth, wired, or Mac speakers into a true Left/Right stereo setup, switch per-app audio sources, adjust volume and balance, swap channels, or run 1-click microphone latency calibration without leaving your keyboard.

## Requirements

- macOS 15 Sequoia or later (Apple Silicon)
- [Consort](https://consortaudio.com) installed (or via Homebrew: `brew install --cask nicolas-gavrilenko/tap/consort`)

## Commands

- **Control Consort**: Inspect your active Left/Right stereo pair, volume, stereo balance, inter-speaker delay, and active audio source from a single view.
- **Switch Audio Source**: Route **All Mac Audio** or isolate a specific app (such as Spotify, Apple Music, or IINA) to your stereo pair.
- **Toggle Stereo Stream**: Start or stop streaming with one keystroke.
- **Toggle Mute**: Mute or unmute Consort stereo output.
- **Increase Volume / Decrease Volume / Set Volume**: Adjust stereo volume by step or jump directly to any percentage (`0` to `100`).
- **Swap Left and Right Speakers**: Instantly exchange Left and Right channels.
- **Sync Audio**: Start 1-click microphone acoustic calibration to align speaker latency.
