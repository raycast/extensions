# Postproxy Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

Manage your social presence across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, X/Twitter,
Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Telegram, and Google Business — straight from Raycast, powered by the
Postproxy API.

- **Status** — one-glance activity snapshot (24h / 7d / 30d) of posts, engagement, comments, reviews,
  and DMs, with deltas vs the previous window and quick jumps into what's awaiting a reply or the
  matching post list (published, scheduled, failed).
- **Profiles** — browse connected profiles grouped by profile group; filter by group, open a
  profile's posts, view lazy per-profile stats, and read & reply to Google Business reviews.
- **Publish Post** — compose a post, target one or more profiles, attach media, schedule it, or save
  it as a draft; pick placements (page / organization / board / channel) and pass raw per-platform
  parameters as JSON.
- **Recent Posts** — browse posts filtered by date, platform, or status; view per-platform results,
  media previews, and Performance Insights; manage comments (reply, hide, like, unlike, delete).
- **Recent Comments** — aggregate comments across all profiles (or a single one) newest-first, reply
  inline, and open the post or comment on the platform.
- **Direct Messages** — browse conversations across all DM-capable profiles (Facebook, Instagram,
  Telegram, Bluesky); read threads, reply, and react.
