# Chinese Lookup Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Type pinyin (`nihao`, `ma3`, `mǎ`, `lv4`) to get characters, words and whole phrases, most likely first, with tones, meanings and traditional forms
- Run-together pinyin (`woxiangxuezhongwen`) is split into the most likely words: 我想学中文
- Phrases get a word-by-word meaning plus Google Translate's translation of the selected phrase
- Type English to find the Chinese words with that meaning, or a sentence to translate it into Chinese with pinyin
- Paste Chinese (simplified or traditional) to see its English translation, pinyin, the other script and every word's meaning
- A single character shows each of its readings with the meaning of each
- Hear any result read aloud, slower on a second press
