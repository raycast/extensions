# Disney

Explore information about your beloved Disney characters by searching for them.

- Browse all characters, or use the dropdown to search by film or TV show title.
- Add or remove favourites with **⌘D**. Saved character details are available offline in **Favourites**, where you can search names, films, and shows.
- Open a character's Disney Wiki page, copy its name with **⌘C**, or copy the wiki link with **⌘⇧C**. Characters without a direct link open a wiki search.
- Use **Browse Films** and **Browse TV Shows** in the action panel, or click an appearance in the details, to explore related characters.
- Reload results with **⌘R** if the service is unavailable.

Character data is provided by the community-run [Disney API](https://disneyapi.dev/). Film and TV show searches match partial titles, so searching “Frozen” can include its sequels. Availability, images, and character appearances depend on that dataset.

Favourites are stored locally on this device. The **Show Images** preference controls character images in the list and details.
