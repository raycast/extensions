# Window Kickflip Changelog

## [Initial Version] - 2026-10-19

- Added **Kickflip**: the focused window lifts off on air thrusters, rolls through a full 360° near the apex, brakes with opposing jets, and lands with a small rebound.
- Added **Kickflip Guide** with instructions, a Do a Kickflip action, and a shortcut to the extension's preferences.
- Added quiet air and touchdown sounds, with a Sound Effects preference to turn them off.
- The animation uses an in-memory snapshot, so the live window never moves or resizes.
- Works with any standard window, including fixed-size windows and windows partly off-screen. No Raycast Pro required.
