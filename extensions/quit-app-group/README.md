# Close Group Apps

## English

**Close Group Apps** is a Raycast extension designed to help you manage your workspace efficiently. It allows you to create a custom group of applications and close them all at once with a single command. This is perfect for switching contexts, ending your work day, or quickly clearing your desktop.

### Use Case Example

Suppose you run apps installed on an external drive and use **MountMate** to manage mounting. If these apps remain open, the drive cannot auto-unmount, preventing your MacBook from sleeping and wasting resources. With this extension, you can instantly quit this specific group of apps before closing your lid, allowing MountMate to safely unmount the drive.

### Features

- **Manage Quit Group**: Easily add or remove applications from your custom group using the "Manage Quit Group" command.
- **One-Click Quit**: Quit all applications in your defined group instantly.
- **Background Execution**: The "Quit App Group" command runs immediately without opening a window, for maximum speed.
- **Safe Quit**: Apps with unsaved changes or those that cannot be quit are skipped to prevent data loss.

## 中文

**Close Group Apps** 是一个 Raycast 插件，旨在帮助你更高效地管理工作区。它允许你创建一个自定义的应用群组，并通过一个指令一次性关闭群组内的所有应用。这非常适合在切换工作场景、结束工作或需要快速清理桌面时使用。

### 使用场景示例

比如你的 MacBook 连接了外置硬盘，并在其中安装了应用。你配合 **MountMate** 自动管理硬盘的挂载和卸载，但如果这些应用一直在运行并占用硬盘，会导致硬盘无法自动卸载，进而阻止 MacBook 休眠并占用内存。此时，使用本插件可以在关闭盖子前一键清退所有设定好的应用组，从而让 MountMate 能够顺利自动卸载外置硬盘。

### 功能特点

- **群组管理**：通过 "Manage Quit Group" 命令，轻松添加或移除自定义群组中的应用。
- **一键退出**：瞬间退出群组内的所有应用。
- **后台运行**："Quit App Group" 命令无需打开窗口即可直接运行，速度极快。
- **安全退出**：如果应用存在未保存的内容或无法退出，插件会跳过该应用而非强制退出，从而避免数据丢失。
