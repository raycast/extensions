# Cactus - Website Blocker

See when Cactus protects your attention, then edit the recurring schedule for your next block without leaving Raycast.

## Install Cactus first

[Cactus for Mac](https://usecactus.app) is a recurring website blocker for macOS 14 or later. Try it free for seven days with no account or card, then buy it once for $19.99 USD. The Raycast extension is included with the same purchase and has no separate charge.

Install Cactus, finish its setup, and enable its background helper before you use the extension. Cactus keeps your schedule and enforcement on your Mac; the extension reads and edits that local state.

## Commands

- **Cactus Status** shows whether protection is active, when the state changes, and which days, hours, and websites are configured.
- **Edit Next Schedule** changes the recurring days, protected hours, and blocked websites.

An active block cannot be changed or stopped from Raycast. If you edit the schedule while Cactus is protecting you, the change applies to the next scheduled block.

## Troubleshooting

If Raycast says Cactus is unavailable, install the latest version from [usecactus.app](https://usecactus.app), open it once, and confirm that the background helper is enabled.

Cactus and this extension do not require an account. Your schedule and blocked websites stay on your Mac.
