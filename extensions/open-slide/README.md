# Open Slide

Search every slide across your deployed [open-slide](https://open-slide.dev) sites without leaving Raycast - speaker notes and page copy included - and open the deck at the page that matched.

Paste the URL of a deployed site and the extension scans it — no API, no config file, nothing to add to your deck repo. Subpath hosting (`base` in `open-slide.config.ts`) works too; paste the full base URL.

## Commands

### Search Slides

Searches titles, themes, speaker notes and page copy across every site you have added, grouped by the folders you arranged in open-slide's own sidebar, with the decks you opened most recently first.
