# Meross Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search Devices: list Meross plugs and power-strip outlets with their on/off state and toggle them
- Toggle Device: pick an online device and toggle it
- Switch Device by Name: switch a device without opening a window, for hotkeys and quicklinks
- Menu Bar Devices: see and switch your plugs from the menu bar
- Log out: end the Meross cloud session
- Local network control with automatic cloud fallback, and support for two-factor authentication
