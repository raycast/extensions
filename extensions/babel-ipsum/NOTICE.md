Open **Information** for those details or use **Copy with Attribution** when exporting protected material. Ordinary Copy and Paste export plain text and do not waive source terms.

## Source terms

Underlying public-domain literary text remains public domain where copyright has expired. This does not establish worldwide clearance for every edition or contributor's work.

Wikisource contributor transcriptions commonly carry CC BY-SA 4.0 terms, sometimes alongside additional notices. Dante's scan-linked transcription records CC BY-SA 3.0/GFDL statements as well as the site's CC BY-SA 4.0 footer. The nineteenth-century Buti/Giannini edition is used rather than Petrocchi. The Korean selection contains only explicitly labelled original columns; its linked scan is a 1951 printing, and fidelity to a 1925 printing has not been verified. Source contributor terms remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

Perseus identifies the Greek edition as Storr 1912 and licenses repository contents under CC BY-SA 4.0 unless otherwise indicated. Critical signs and bracketed readings are preserved; no independent full scan collation is claimed.

Project Gutenberg identifies the included author texts as public domain in the United States. Its [reuse terms](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html) and [permission policy](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html) apply as described by that source; the name is used only in acknowledgements. Those statements do not establish worldwide status.

Aozora Bunko's [handling rules](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html) permit redistribution and adaptation of expired-copyright works. Catalog metadata separately carries CC BY 4.0; this does not license every site asset.

Hindawi states that its 2012 book text is public domain. Safahat's rights-reserved footer does not grant reuse of site assets, interface, or editorial material, which are excluded.

Project editorial adaptations of Wikisource or Perseus material, including Sub terra modernization, are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0 to the extent the project holds rights. Third-party rights retain their applicable terms. See the bundled [corpus licensing scope](assets/corpus-license.txt), separate from the software's [MIT license](LICENSE).

Detailed source evidence, preparation records, and limitations are available in the [pinned repository corpus notice](https://github.com/frovere/babel-ipsum/blob/3897a81732fa88ade7c72a0c72fe8c93de9b571f/corpus/NOTICE.md) and [per-book metadata](https://github.com/frovere/babel-ipsum/blob/3897a81732fa88ade7c72a0c72fe8c93de9b571f/corpus/books/). The runtime database records the actual bundled selection; the repository also retains development inputs that are not included in this extension.
