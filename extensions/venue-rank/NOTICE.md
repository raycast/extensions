## ICORE

The bundled ICORE2026 assessment facts were retrieved from the public Computing Research and Education / ICORE conference portal at https://portal.core.edu.au/conf-ranks/ on 2026-11-10. The snapshot contains 987 records from 20 result pages, including original labels and per-record source URLs. The raw snapshot records the page URLs and content hashes.

The portal identifies its publisher as Computing Research & Education. Its original material remains subject to the publisher's terms. The project's MIT license applies to project code and does not grant additional rights over third-party material. Venue Rank does not redistribute the portal's HTML, logos, reviews, or submissions.
