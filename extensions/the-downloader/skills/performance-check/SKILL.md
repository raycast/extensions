# Performance check

1. Call The Downloader's `get-link-info` tool once per URL.
2. Use the numbers in the **Statistics** section: for videos, views, likes, comments, channel subscribers, age in days, views per day, likes per 100 views and comments per 1,000 views; for posts, likes (or Reddit's score), comments and images, with the publish date.
3. Report:
   - A short verdict in one sentence.
   - The key figures, as a table when comparing more than one link (columns such as: link, age, views, views/day, likes per 100 views, comments per 1,000 views — leave out what the site doesn't report).
   - What stands out: e.g. views per day far above or below the others, engagement much higher than views suggest, views relative to the channel's subscriber count.
4. Be careful with benchmarks. Only compare against the other links given, or the channel's subscriber count. Don't invent "typical" rates; if you mention a rule of thumb, call it rough general knowledge.
5. Say when a figure is missing (some sites hide views, likes or comments) instead of treating it as zero.
6. For what the video or post is about, also call `read-link`.

Reply in the language the person wrote in.
