# Holy Quran

Experience the Holy Quran with beautiful recitations directly in Raycast.

## Features
- **Browse Surahs**: View all 114 Surahs with detailed metadata (Arabic name, translation, Ayah count, and revelation place).
- **Reciter Selection**: Choose from hundreds of world-renowned reciters.
- **Memorization Mode**: Focus on specific Ayah ranges with custom repeat counts (1x up to Infinite).
- **Background Playback**: Continue listening to the divine verses even after closing the Raycast window.
- **Menu Bar Integration**: See the currently playing Surah and reciter at a glance, with quick access to stop playback.
- **Favorites**: Pave your way by favoriting Surahs and Reciters for quick access.

## Commands
- **Play Surah**: The main hub for browsing and playing recitations.
- **Memorization Mode**: specialized tool for Hifz (available via `Cmd+M` on any Surah).
- **Stop Recitation**: Instantly silence all audio.
- **Set Default Reciter**: Pick your favorite reciter for one-click playback.
- **List Reciters**: Explore the vast library of available voices.

## Usage
1. Open **Play Surah**.
2. Search for your desired Surah.
3. Press **Enter** to play with your default reciter, or **Cmd+M** for Memorization Mode.
4. Manage everything from your Mac Menu Bar.
