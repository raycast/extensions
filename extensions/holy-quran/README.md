# Holy Quran

<img src="extension-icon.png" width="100" align="left" alt="Extension Logo" />

Experience the Divine Message with world-class recitations directly on your Mac. The **Holy Quran** extension for Raycast provides a seamless, immersive, and highly configurable listening experience designed for both casual listening and dedicated memorization.

<br clear="left" />

## Core Features

### 🎧 Seamless Playback
- **Browse & Search**: Instantly find any of the 114 Surahs by name (English or Arabic), number, or revelation place (Meccan/Medinan).
- **Background Audio**: Built on a robust background engine that keeps the recitation playing even after you close Raycast.
- **HUD Notifications**: Non-intrusive floating messages for quick actions like Pausing, Resuming, or Stopping.

### 📖 Enhanced Memorization (Hifz) Mode
- **Precision Ranges**: Select a specific range of Ayahs (e.g., Ayah 1-5) to focus your study.
- **Intelligent Looping**: Toggle **Infinite Looping** for continuous repetition or set a specific count (up to 20x).
- **Live Repeat Toggle**: Change your mind mid-play! Toggle the repeat state ON or OFF while listening, and the engine will adapt gracefully at the end of the current verse.
- **Integrated Selection**: Choose your reciter directly within the memorization form.

### 🍏 Premium Menu Bar Integration
- **Real-Time Status**: View the currently playing Surah and elapsed/remaining time right in your Menu Bar.
- **Global Controls**: Pause, Resume, Stop, or Toggle Repeat directly from the Menu Bar menu without opening the main Raycast window.
- **Quick Launcher**: Rapidly jump back into searching or start a new memorization session.

### 🎙️ Global Reciters Library
- **Massive Library**: Access hundreds of world-renowned reciter-styles including Mishary Rashid Alafasy, AbdulBaset AbdulSamad, Al-Minshawi, and many more.
- **Default Preferences**: Set your favorite reciter globally for "One-Click" playback.

### ⚡ Performance & Offline Support
- **Smart Caching**: Once a Surah or verse range is played, it's cached locally for instant startup and offline access.
- **Fast Connectivity Checks**: Optimized network logic ensures the UI stays snappy even on unstable connections.

## Commands

- **Search Surahs**: Browse all Surahs with detailed metadata.
- **Memorize Surah**: Open the dedicated form to configure focal points and loops.
- **Stop Recitation**: Instantly kill all background audio processes.
- **Pause/Resume**: Quickly toggle your playback state.
- **Toggle Repeat**: Turn continuous looping ON or OFF in real-time.
- **Set Default Reciter**: Your one-stop shop for setting global preferences.
- **List Reciters**: Browse the extensive list of available voices and styles.

## Usage Tips

1. **Quicklinks**: Use the `Play Surah` command with arguments to create your own morning/evening "Quicklinks" (e.g., `surah: 67`).
2. **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Open the Menu Bar status to see current shortcuts (typically `Cmd+Shift+P` for Pause).
3. **Single Ayah**: In Memorization mode, leave the "End Ayah" blank to loop a single specific verse.
